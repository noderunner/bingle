# Solitaire (Klondike)

The game most computers have called Solitaire since 1990. It is old: nobody knows who first played it, and the name is said to come from the Klondike gold rush.

## The aim

Move all 52 cards to the four **foundations**, one per suit, each built up from ace to king.

## The table

* Seven **columns** of 1 to 7 cards, with only the top card of each face up.
* The **stock**, face down, and the **waste** beside it.
* The four foundations, empty at the start.

## A move

* **In a column** cards go down in alternating colours: a black 6 goes on a red 7. A face-up card can be moved with everything on top of it, as long as they are in that order.
* **An empty column** takes only a king.
* **A card uncovered** in a column turns face up by itself.
* **The stock**: click it to turn over one card onto the waste. The waste's top card can be played to a column or a foundation.
* **When the stock is empty**, click it to turn the waste over into a new stock, as often as you like.
* **A foundation's top card** can come back to a column, which now and then is exactly what you need.

## Playing here

Click a card, then where it should go. If it can go to only one place, it goes at once. You can also drag it. Click a selected card again to send it to its foundation. **Finish** appears once everything can go home by itself. **← Back** takes a move back.

With a themed set of cards the suits have other faces, so each card also carries a small red or black mark, showing which colour it counts as.

## Advice

Uncover face-down cards before anything else. Think before moving a king into an empty column: choose the one that frees the most. Do not rush cards to the foundations if they might be needed to build on.
