# Solitaire (draw three)

Klondike as the casinos dealt it, and the harder way to play. Everything is as in the one-card game, with one difference.

## The aim

Move all 52 cards to the four **foundations**, one per suit, each built up from ace to king.

## The difference

Clicking the stock turns over **three** cards onto the waste, or as many as are left, and only the top one can be played. Playing it frees the one beneath.

So you see only every third card on a pass through the stock, and the order changes only when you play from the waste. Unlimited passes are allowed, but they come round the same cards until you do.

## Everything else

* **In a column** cards go down in alternating colours, and a face-up card can be moved with everything in order on top of it.
* **An empty column** takes only a king.
* **A card uncovered** in a column turns face up by itself.
* **A foundation's top card** can come back to a column.

Click a card, then where it should go, or drag it. **Finish** appears once everything can go home by itself, and **← Back** takes a move back.
