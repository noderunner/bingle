# Spider

A patience game with two decks' worth of cards, 104 in all, known since at least the 1940s. The four-suit game is hard, two suits is a fair fight, and one suit is the place to learn it.

## The aim

Build complete runs from king down to ace **in one suit**. A finished run leaves the table by itself. Clear all eight and you have won.

## The table

Ten **columns**: six cards in each of the first four and five in the others, with only the top card face up. The other 50 cards are the **stock**, five deals of ten.

## A move

* **Any card can go on a card one rank higher**, whatever its suit.
* **Only cards of one suit in order can be moved together.** A run with the wrong suit in it is stuck until you break it up. That is the whole difficulty of the game.
* **An empty column** takes anything.
* **A card uncovered** in a column turns face up by itself.
* **The stock**: click it to deal one card onto every column. You cannot do this while a column is empty.

Click a card, then where it should go, or drag it. **← Back** takes a move back.

## Advice

Build in suit whenever you can. An empty column is worth more than almost anything: use it to untangle runs, then empty it again. Before you deal, get as many cards in order as you can, because a deal covers every column.
