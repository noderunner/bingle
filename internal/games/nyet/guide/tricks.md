# Teams, tricks and scoring

## Teams

The first player chooses their team; everyone else forms the opposing team.

- **Four players:** two against two — the leader names a partner.
- **Two players:** one against one, no choice.
- **Three players:** one against two — the leader plays **alone** or with **one partner**. The player who plays alone gets the **×2 bonus card**.
- **Five players:** two against three or three against two, as the leader chooses. The leader gives the ×2 bonus card to a player on the **two-player** team (it can be themselves, or an opponent).

The bonus card doubles its owner's points in this round only.

## Playing a trick

The first player leads any card; play goes round the table.

- You **must follow the colour led** if you can.
- If you cannot, you may play **any card** — including a trump or a super trump.
- A super trump counts as the **trump colour**, so it follows trump (and must be played when trump is led and you have no other way to follow).

## Who takes the trick

1. If a **super trump** was played, it takes the trick.
2. Otherwise, if any **trump** was played, the highest trump takes it.
3. Otherwise the highest card of the **colour led** takes it.

When two cards of the same colour and value are played (the three 1s), **the last one played wins** — also among trumps and super trumps. The winner leads the next trick.

## Loot

Every **1** can be captured. If you win a trick that holds a 1 played by a player of the **other team**, you claim that card as **loot** (shown face up beside your tricks). A 1 played by your own teammate is not loot.

## Scoring a round

Card values do not matter. Each team counts **its tricks plus its loot**, multiplies by the number in the Points box, and *every player on the team* scores that. The player with the bonus card doubles their own score.

::: example
Points is 2. The team of Wolf and Tiger took 7 tricks and captured 2 loot cards: 9 × 2 = 18 points each. Tiger holds the bonus card (the team was the smaller one): Tiger scores 36.
:::

After the last round the highest total wins; a tie at the top is a draw.
