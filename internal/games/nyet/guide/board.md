# The board of terms

The board has five lines. In the Nyet! phase, players take turns covering one box with a token — **a choice they do not want**. A line with only one open box is settled and takes no more tokens. When every line is settled, **the open box of each line is the rule of the round**. (Boxes of characters who are not playing are covered from the start, for the whole game.)

| Line | The open box decides |
|---|---|
| **First player** | who leads the first trick, chooses the team and gives out the bonus card |
| **Discard** | what happens to cards before the tricks — see below |
| **Trump** | the trump colour (red, blue, yellow or green) |
| **Super trump** | the colour whose three 1s are super trumps, or *Nyet!*: none |
| **Points** | what each trick and each captured 1 is worth: 1, 2, 3, 4 or **−2** |

## The Discard line

- **Nyet!** — nobody discards.
- **1** or **2** — every player discards that many cards, face down. They are out of the round and score nothing.
- **except 1** — every player discards one card that is *not* a 1.
- **pass left** — every player chooses a card and passes it to the player on their left (the next player in turn order). Everyone chooses before any card changes hands.

## Super trumps

The three 1s of the Super-trump colour are **super trumps**. In the trick phase they turn into a **double agent**: whatever their printed colour, they count as the **trump** colour — and beat every other trump. If trump is led, you must play a trump or a super trump if you can.

::: tip
A negative Points box (−2) turns the game upside down: now winning tricks and capturing loot *costs* points. Cross the boxes you dread.
:::
