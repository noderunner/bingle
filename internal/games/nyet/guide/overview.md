# Nyet!

Stefan Dorra's trick-taking game for two to five players. What makes it different: before every round, the players take turns **crossing out** choices on a board — each one saying what they do *not* want — and whatever is left sets the rules of the round: who leads, what is discarded, which colour is trump, which cards are super trumps and what a trick is worth. Then the leader chooses a team and the tricks are played.

## The game in short

1. **Deal.** All the cards are dealt out (15 each with 2 or 4 players, 16 with 3, 12 with 5).
2. **The Nyet! phase.** Starting with the dealer, every player crosses off one box of [the board](tab:board) until only one box is left on each line. Look at your hand first: you will want trump in the colour you hold, and a trick worth many points if you expect to win.
3. **Teams.** The first player chooses a team; see [teams](tab:tricks).
4. **Discards.** If the Discard line says so, cards are discarded or passed to the left.
5. **The tricks.** Follow the colour led, trump when you cannot, and win tricks and capture 1s as loot.
6. **Scoring.** Each team scores its tricks and loot at the round's points value, then a new round starts.

After a fixed number of rounds — **8** with 2 or 4 players, **9** with 3, **10** with 5 — the player with the most points wins.

## The cards

60 cards in four colours — **red, blue, yellow and green** — with the values **1 (three of each colour)** and 2 to 13. With three players the 11, 12 and 13 of every colour are taken out (48 cards). Values count only for winning tricks; they never score.

## In the game

Your hand is at the bottom, fanned: the cards you may play glow, click one to play it. The other players' cards are shown face down. On the board, boxes you may cross glow too; a crossed box shows who crossed it. The cards in the middle are the trick being played and, beside it, the trick before.
