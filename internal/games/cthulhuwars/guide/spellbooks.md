# Spellbooks

Every cult has **six spellbooks** and **six requirements**. When you meet a requirement you gain *one* spellbook of your choice from your faction card (a book prompt appears). Six books are needed to win by Doom, and with six you may Battle as an unlimited action.

> [!NOTE]
> The texts follow the current faction pages of the Cthulhu Wars wiki (with the rules errata and updates). A spellbook marked **reaction** may be used on another player's turn: the game asks its owner after each other player's action — see *Reactions* in the Actions page.

## Great Cthulhu

### Spellbooks

| Spellbook | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Absorb** | Pre-Battle | If a Shoggoth is in the battle, eliminate any number of your monsters or cultists there; for each, the Shoggoth rolls 3 more dice. |
| **Devolve** | Ongoing — reaction | After any player's Action — even when it is not your turn — you may replace any number of your Acolyte Cultists on the map with Deep Ones from your pool. It costs nothing. |
| **Dreams** | Action: Cost 2 | Choose an area holding an enemy Acolyte Cultist: that enemy eliminates one of its Acolytes there and you place one of yours. |
| **Regenerate** | Post-Battle | Assign up to 2 Kill or Pain results to the same Star Spawn. If 2 Kills are applied it dies; any other combination only Pains it. |
| **Submerge** | Action: Cost 1 | If Cthulhu is in an ocean, take him — and any of your units there that you choose — off the map onto your faction card. Later, for 0 Power, put them all into any area. |
| **Y'ha-Nthlei** | Gather Power | If Cthulhu is in play, gain 1 Power for each enemy-controlled Gate in an ocean area. |

### Requirements

1. Receive this spellbook in the first Doom phase, and **1 Elder Sign**.
2. Kill and/or Devour an enemy unit in a battle.
3. Kill and/or Devour 2 enemy units in a battle. (Both kill requirements are met in one battle by killing or devouring 3 or more.)
4. Control 3 Gates in ocean areas, or 4 Gates exist in ocean areas.
5. Awaken Cthulhu.
6. This must be the last spellbook you receive, taken in a Doom phase — and **1 Elder Sign**.

**Immortal:** whenever you awaken *any* Great Old One you gain 1 Elder Sign, and after the first time Cthulhu costs only 4 Power. He can only be awakened in your starting area (the South Pacific), which must hold a Gate — yours, abandoned or an enemy's.

## Crawling Chaos

### Spellbooks

| Spellbook | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Abduct** | Pre-Battle | Eliminate one or more of your Nightgaunts from the battle; for each one the enemy must eliminate one of its Monsters or Cultists there. |
| **Invisibility** | Pre-Battle | For each Flying Polyp present, choose one Monster or Cultist in the battle — yours or an enemy's: it takes no part in the rest of the battle. |
| **Seek and Destroy** | Pre-Battle | Immediately move any or all Hunting Horrors from any area into the battle area. |
| **Emissary of the Outer Gods** | Post-Battle | Unless an enemy Great Old One is in the battle, a Kill applied to Nyarlathotep becomes a Pain; if he cannot be Pained because he is surrounded he is not eliminated. |
| **Madness** | Post-Battle — reaction | After all Pains are assigned, you choose where the pained units go, in any order, in **every** battle — even those you are not in. They still may not enter the area of the faction they fought. |
| **The Thousand Forms** | Action: Cost 0 | Once per Action phase, if Nyarlathotep is in play, roll a die: your enemies lose that much Power between them. If they cannot pay it all, you gain that much Power instead. |

### Requirements

1. As your action, pay 4 Power.
2. As your action, pay 6 Power. (Both requirements can be met in one action by paying 10.)
3. Control 3 Gates, or have 12 Power.
4. Control 4 Gates, or have 15 Power.
5. Capture an enemy Cultist.
6. Awaken Nyarlathotep.

**The Harbinger:** when enemy Great Old Ones are Pained or Killed in a battle Nyarlathotep is in, you gain Power equal to half their awakening cost — or 2 Elder Signs for each instead. His Combat is the number of spellbooks on your faction card plus those of your enemy in the battle.

## Black Goat

### Spellbooks

| Spellbook | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Blood Sacrifice** | Doom Phase | If Shub-Niggurath is in play you may eliminate one of your Cultists to gain 1 Elder Sign. |
| **Frenzy** | Ongoing | Your Cultists have 1 Combat (they can still be captured). |
| **Ghroth** | Action: Cost 2 | Roll a die. If it is no more than the number of areas holding your Fungi, your enemies between them eliminate that many Cultists (they share it out; if they cannot agree you choose); otherwise you place any faction's Acolyte anywhere on the map. |
| **The Red Sign** | Ongoing | Your Dark Young can create and control Gates (they act as Cultists for nothing else). Each adds 1 to Shub-Niggurath's Combat and provides 1 Power in Gather Power. |
| **Necrophagy** | Post-Battle — reaction | Move any or all Ghouls that did not take part in the battle into the battle area — even in a battle you are not in. Each side suffers an extra Pain for each Ghoul moved (not on the Ghouls just called). |
| **The Thousand Young** | Ongoing | While Shub-Niggurath is in play, Ghouls, Fungi and Dark Young each cost 1 less to summon. |

### Requirements

1. Have units in 4 areas.
2. Have units in 6 areas.
3. Have units in 8 areas.
4. As your action, eliminate 2 of your Cultists from anywhere on the map.
5. Share areas with all enemies (you and each enemy have units in some area — not necessarily the same one). Gain 1 Power per enemy.
6. Awaken Shub-Niggurath.

**Avatar** (Shub-Niggurath's action, 1 Power): choose an area and a faction — Shub-Niggurath swaps places with a Monster or Cultist of that faction there, which its owner picks.

## Yellow Sign

### Spellbooks

| Spellbook | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Passion** | Ongoing | When one or more of your Cultists are eliminated by an enemy, gain 1 Power. |
| **He Who is Not to be Named** | Action: Cost 1 | Move Hastur to any area containing a Cultist of any faction. You may then take a second, different action — not The Screaming Dead. |
| **The Third Eye** | Ongoing | While Hastur is in play, Desecrating costs 1 less; if it succeeds you also gain 1 Elder Sign. |
| **Shriek of the Byakhee** | Action: Cost 1 | Move any or all Byakhee from where they stand to one area on the map. |
| **The Screaming Dead** | Action: Cost 1 | Move the King in Yellow to an adjacent area; any of the Undead with him may come along for free. You may then take a second, different action — not He Who is Not to be Named. |
| **Zingaya** | Action: Cost 1 | Choose an area with your Undead and an enemy Cultist: that enemy eliminates one of its Cultists there and you place an Undead from your pool. |

### Requirements

1. Awaken the King in Yellow.
2. Successfully Desecrate an area marked with the Dragon glyph.
3. Successfully Desecrate an area marked with the Chevron glyph.
4. Successfully Desecrate an area marked with the Thorns glyph.
5. Awaken Hastur — and gain **1 Elder Sign**.
6. As your action, choose another player to gain 3 Doom.

## Windwalker

### Spellbooks

| Spellbook | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Arctic Wind** | Ongoing | When Ithaqua uses the Move action, any or all your units in the same area can move with him for no additional cost. |
| **Berserkergang** | Post-Battle | For each Gnoph-Keh assigned a Kill in battle, the enemy must eliminate one of its own Monsters or Cultists (its choice, anywhere on the map). |
| **Cannibalism** | Post-Battle — reaction | After all battle results have been applied, if one or more enemy units were killed, you may place a Wendigo or Acolyte from your pool into the battle area — even if you were not in the battle. |
| **Herald of the Outer Gods** | Ongoing | Pay 5 Power for Windwalker's Ritual of Annihilation, whatever the Ritual track says. |
| **Howl** | Pre-Battle | Before a battle, if any Wendigo is present, you may force the enemy to retreat one unit (its choice) out of the area into an adjacent one. It is not a Pain: the unit may go to an area that holds your units. |
| **Ice Age** | Action: Cost 1 | Place your Ice Age token in any area. When an enemy takes an Action that ends in that area it must spend 1 Power more. |

### Requirements

1. Awaken Rhan-Tegoth.
2. A Gate exists in the area marked with the Windwalker sigil in which you did not start.
3. You are the First Player.
4. Another faction has 6 spellbooks on its faction card.
5. Take this spellbook at any time. Gain 1 Elder Sign for each enemy with 6 spellbooks, to a maximum of 3.
6. Awaken Ithaqua.

**Hibernate** is the cult's unique ability (see the Actions page). **Ferox**: while Ithaqua is in play, your Cultists
cannot be captured by enemy Monsters (Great Old Ones still can). **Eternal** (Rhan-Tegoth): when he receives a Pain or
a Kill you may pay 1 Power to cancel it; he can only receive one result per battle.

## Sleeper

### Spellbooks

| Spellbook | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Ancient Sorcery** | Action: Cost 1 | Remove a Serpent Man from the map and put him on an enemy's faction card: until the end of the next Doom phase you have that faction's unique ability (where it names a Great Old One it includes Tsathoggua). Then gain 1 Power and put the Serpent Man anywhere on the map. |
| **Burrow** | Ongoing | After a Move action in which you spent 2 or more Power moving units, regain 1 Power. |
| **Capture Monster** | Action: Cost 1 | Tsathoggua can capture enemy Monsters the way Cultists are captured. Each is sacrificed for 1 Power in the next Gather Power phase. |
| **Cursed Slumber** | Action: Cost 1 | Remove one of your controlled Gates and its Cultist from the map onto your faction card: they still give Doom and Power but are immune to enemy abilities and spellbooks. For 1 Power, as an Action, put them back into any area without a Gate. Only one Gate may sleep at a time. |
| **Demand Sacrifice** | Pre-Battle | If Tsathoggua is in play, before a battle with you your enemy chooses: you gain an Elder Sign, or all its Kill results against your units in this battle count as Pains. |
| **Energy Nexus** | Ongoing — reaction | Just before a battle in an area that holds a Wizard, you may take one Action that starts in that area, for its normal Power cost. The battle then goes on from its Pre-Battle abilities. |

### Requirements

1. As your Action, spend 3 Power. Each other player gains 1 Power.
2. As your Action, spend 3 Power. Select another player, who gains 3 Power.
3. As your Action, spend 3 Power. Each other player loses 1 Power.
4. Roll 6 combat dice in a single battle.
5. Perform a Ritual of Annihilation.
6. Awaken Tsathoggua.

**Death from Below** is the cult's unique ability (Doom phase). **Lethargy** (Tsathoggua): Action, cost 0 — do nothing,
and it counts as an Action. **Ancient Sorcery** lends the Sleeper, for example, Flight, Immortal, Fertility Cult,
Feast, or Hibernate while the Serpent Man lies on that faction's card; each cult can hold only one of them at a time.

## Opener of the Way

### Spellbooks

| Spellbook | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Channel Power** | Battle | After rolling Battle dice, you may pay 1 Power to reroll all dice that were not Kills or Pains. You may do this more than once. |
| **Dragon Ascending** | Once only | Once during the game, at any time, set your Power equal to the current Power of one chosen enemy. (Offered in your turn — also with no Power left — and in the Doom phase.) |
| **Dragon Descending** | Once only | Once during the game when you perform a Ritual of Annihilation, you receive twice the normal Doom. |
| **Dread Curse of Azathoth** | Action: Cost 2 | Select an area and roll 1 die for each Abomination and Spawn of Yog-Sothoth in play. Apply the Kills and Pains to enemy factions in the area: you choose which factions receive which results, they choose which of their units takes each, and you choose the area each Pained unit goes to. No Battle abilities apply. |
| **The Million Favored Ones** | Post-Battle | After Pains and Kills are resolved, replace any or all surviving Acolytes with Mutants, Mutants with Abominations and Abominations with Spawns of Yog-Sothoth. A Spawn can be replaced by as many Mutants as are in your pool. |
| **They Break Through** | Ongoing | You can summon Monsters at enemy-controlled and abandoned Gates. You need no units in the area. |

### Requirements

1. There are 8 Gates on the map (Yog-Sothoth counts as one).
2. There are 12 Gates on the map (10 in a 2–3 player game).
3. You have units in at least 2 areas that hold enemy-controlled Gates.
4. Lose 1 of your own units in a battle.
5. Your Great Old One is in the same area as an enemy Great Old One.
6. Awaken Yog-Sothoth.

## Tcho-Tcho

### Spellbooks (the Tsang tribe)

| Spellbook | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Hierophants** | Ongoing | When you earn a faction spellbook (this one too), place a High Priest at one of your Gates; with none in your pool, Ubbo-Sathla's Growth counter advances by 1 instead. When this book is first taken with the High Priests option, every faction places a High Priest at one of its Gates. |
| **Soulless** | Ongoing | When your captured Cultists are sacrificed they provide 0 Power. |
| **Terror** | Battle | Choose one: your enemy's Combat is reduced by 1 per Proto-Shoggoth in the battle, or yours is increased by 1 per Proto-Shoggoth. |
| **Idolatry** | Action: Cost 1 | Select an area containing another faction's starting glyph (even a faction not in play). Move any or all of your units from adjacent areas into it. |

2. Earn an Elder Sign.
3. Own 3 or more Elder Signs.
4. As an Action, remove your controlled Gate in your start area.
5. Any Great Old One is Killed in a battle.
6. Awaken Ubbo-Sathla.

## High Priests

With the option, every cult may sacrifice its High Priest with **Unspeakable Oath** — see the Actions and Factions
pages. He is a Cultist like the Acolytes for every requirement and spellbook that names Cultists, and a unit for
those that name units (Black Goat's areas, for one).

## Neutral and Independent spellbooks

Two kinds of spellbook belong to no cult's own list of six.

- The six **neutral spellbooks** (Shriveling, Stars Are Right, Recriminations, The Mao Ceremony, Umr at-Tawil, Undimensioned)
  are taken *instead of* one of a cult's own books, count as one of its six, and can be held by one cult at a time — see
  [Neutral](tab:neutral).
- Each **Independent Great Old One** has a spellbook of its own that lies on its Loyalty Card, is earned by its own
  requirement, **does not count** among the cult's six and falls off when the Independent dies:

| Independent | Spellbook | When | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![](piece:abhoth:green) Abhoth | **The Brood** | Ongoing | 4+ different Monster types in play (Filth included), or 8+ Monsters in all |
| ![](piece:chaugnar-faugn:green) Chaugnar Faugn | **Curse of Chaugnar Faugn** | Ongoing | As your Action, discard 2 Elder Signs |
| ![](piece:cthugha:green) Cthugha | **Firestorm** | Post-Battle | Kill an enemy Great Old One in battle |
| ![](piece:mother-hydra:green) Mother Hydra | **The Zygote** | Action: Cost 1 | Control no Great Old Ones in ocean areas, or the enemies control none |
| ![](piece:yig:green) Yig | **Messenger of Yig** | Ongoing | As your Action, remove one of your controlled Gates from the map |

See [Independents](tab:independents) for what each does.
