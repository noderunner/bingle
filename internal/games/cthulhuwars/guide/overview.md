# Cthulhu Wars

Three to five cults race to wake their gods, summon monsters and rack up **Doom** while the stars come right.
Be first to **30 Doom with all six spellbooks** — or bring the whole world to the brink and be the one
cult with the most Doom when the game ends in *Instant Death*.

![The world map|760](board.svg "The world map of this game: its areas, six oceans and the glyphs on the continents that Yellow Sign needs.")

## Setting up a table

When you create the game you choose

- **the layout** — three, four or five players, each with its own map (see [the map](tab:map));
- **the cults** — every seat of the table has a list of the six cults, and no cult can be chosen twice. A table left as
  it came keeps the layout's own cults: the four core cults, plus the Windwalker as the fifth at a five-player table.
  Choosing a cult that sits in another seat trades the two;
- **the High Priests** — a check box: with it, every cult has one [High Priest](tab:factions), a Cultist that can be
  recruited for 3 Power and sacrificed for 2;
- **the neutral cards** — check boxes for the Azathoth faction (neutral spellbooks and Monsters), the Dreamlands
  Surface Monsters, Beyond Time and Space (two Monsters and a Terror) and each of the five Independent Great Old Ones
  of Great Old One Pack 1 (see [Neutral](tab:neutral) and [Independents](tab:independents)).

The Windwalker begins first of all: it chooses which of its two sigil areas gets its Gate and Cultists. The first
player of the game — **Great Cthulhu wherever it sits, or at a table without it the cult first by name** (Black Goat,
Crawling Chaos, Great Cthulhu, Sleeper, Windwalker, Yellow Sign) — then chooses the direction of play, and the first
Action phase begins (the Doom phase is skipped in the first round; see [The round](tab:round)).

## How a round plays

1. **Action phase** — in turn order, each player takes **one action** and spends Power on it, until nobody can (or
   wants to) act. Players with no Power left are skipped.
2. **Gather Power** — 1 Power per Cultist in play, 2 per Gate you control, 1 per abandoned Gate (for everyone).
3. **First player** — whoever has the most Power goes first next round and picks the direction of play. (In the
   first round of the game it is Great Cthulhu, or the first cult by name when it is not at the table.)
4. **Doom phase** — each Gate you control scores 1 **Doom**; then you may perform the **Ritual of Annihilation**
   and reveal **Elder Signs**.

Read [The round](tab:round) for the details, [Actions](tab:actions) for what you can do with your Power,
and [Battle](tab:battle) for how fights are resolved.

## What you need to know first

- **Power is everything.** It buys every action. Cultists and Gates feed it; running out ends your turn.
- **Gates are your engine** — they give Power, Doom, a place to summon and a place to awaken your Great Old Ones.
- **Spellbooks win games.** Each of your six spellbook *requirements* is a goal; meeting one earns a book of your
  choice. You need all six to win by Doom.
- **Battles are dice.** A 6 is a Kill, a 4 or 5 is a Pain. The winner of a fight is whoever keeps their units.

## Reading the screen

![](piece:cultist:green) a **Cultist** &nbsp; ![](piece:deep-one:green) a **monster** &nbsp; ![](piece:cthulhu:green) a **Great Old One**.
A small number on a token is how many identical units stand there.

A **Gate** is a dark portal among the units of its area, ringed in the colour of the cult that controls it; the figure
drawn on it is the unit that occupies it — an Acolyte, the High Priest or a Dark Young — and it is not counted in the
stack of its kind beside the Gate. Click it to move it: that abandons the Gate. A dashed grey ring is an abandoned Gate.
A Gate appears only where one has been built. A ✠ marks a **Desecrated** area, a ❄ chip the area of
Windwalker's **Ice Age** token, a round badge after a name a **glyph**, and a coloured diamond before it the **sigil** of
the Windwalker or the Sleeper. A red outline is the battle in progress. The **Key** tab of this window shows each of
them, and the game options can switch the names, glyphs and sigils off.

See [Playing the game](tab:playing) for how to enter moves.
