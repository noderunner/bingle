# Independent Great Old Ones

An **Independent Great Old One** belongs to no cult. Whoever has a Great Old One of its own standing with a Gate it controls may wake one — and then it is theirs until it dies. Great Old One Pack 1 brings five: **Abhoth**, **Chaugnar Faugn**, **Cthugha**, **Mother Hydra** and **Yig**.

Switch them on at the setup page with the check boxes *Independent: …* (any number of them; the wiki recommends, for a first game, **one Independent fewer than there are players**). Azathoth himself, the Independent of the Azathoth faction, is not in the game — only that faction's neutral spellbooks and Monsters (see [Neutral](tab:neutral)).

## The rules

As printed on the Independents' Loyalty Card:

> **Awakening:** Take the Independent's Loyalty Card, Spellbook, and any tokens, and add them to your Pool. Place the Independent's figure on the Map, under your Control. There are no limits to how many Independents you may Control, and you may use one Independent to help you Awaken another.
>
> **Death:** If your Independent is Killed, place its Loyalty Card, figure, unused tokens, and Spellbook back into the general Pool (tokens already on the map remain there). If you had earned its Spellbook, it "falls off" the Loyalty Card and is no longer in effect. If this Independent is Awakened again, even by the same player, the Spellbook must be earned anew.
>
> **Spellbook:** Each Independent has its own Spellbook to be earned. When a Spellbook's requirements have been met, place the Spellbook on the Independent's Loyalty Card; you may reap that Spellbook's benefits as long as you control that Independent. This Spellbook does not count as one of your "Faction" Spellbooks for any purpose, and it cannot be placed on your Faction Card.
>
> **Doom Phase:** When you perform a Ritual of Annihilation, do NOT gain an Elder Sign for any Independent Great Old Ones you Control.
>
> **Note:** For your first game with Independents, we recommend using one fewer Independent than the number of players.

### In Bingle

- **Awakening** is an Action like any other: the button *Awaken Abhoth (4)* (the Power is on it) appears when you control a **Gate in an area where you also have a Great Old One** — one of your cult's or another Independent of yours (the Opener's Yog-Sothoth is a Gate and a Great Old One in one). Press it, then the area. The card, its spellbook and its tokens are yours; the figure stands at the Gate.
- Great Cthulhu's **Immortal** gives an Elder Sign for *any* Great Old One awakened — an Independent too.
- **The card lies in your *Loyalty Cards* panel**, with its requirement and its spellbook; the spellbook is *not* in your *Spellbooks 4/6* counter and cannot be set aside by Recriminations or lie under a Moonbeast. Where a requirement is a state of the map it is met — and the book earned — as soon as it is true; the others are Actions or events.
- **Death** is any elimination of the figure: a Kill in a battle, a Dread Curse, being replaced by Cthugha. Its card goes back to the pool, the book falls off, the tokens you had not placed go back; anyone who has a Great Old One at a Gate may then wake it.
- **No Elder Signs in the Ritual** for Independents: the Ritual gives one per Great Old One *of your cult*.
- An Independent counts as a Great Old One for everything else: it holds the cult's units, can be hit by the Harbinger, Devour cannot touch it, an enemy cult counts it for Hibernate and Yog-Sothoth's Combat, killing one meets the Tcho-Tcho's requirement, and **an Elder Thing next to it silences its ability** (not its spellbook).

### Abhoth

![](piece:abhoth:green) *The living pool of eternally-spawning loathsomeness, ultimate source of all earth life.*

**Awaken:** 1. Your controlled Gate is in an area with your Great Old One. 2. Pay 4 Power, and place Abhoth in the area containing the Gate. **Combat:** the number of Filth Tokens in play (0–12).

**Filth** (Action: Cost 1): place a Filth Token in any area. Twelve ![](piece:filth:green) Filth Tokens come with Abhoth (the ones not already on the map). Filth Tokens act as Combat 0 Monsters. They belong to Abhoth's faction, but never move nor take Actions. They can be Killed or Pained normally in battle, and are affected by spellbooks and abilities (so they protect Cultists against capture like any Monster, and Shriveling or Absorb can take them). *In Bingle the Filth is placed with one click on the area; it is never summoned or moved — not by Move, Undimensioned or Idolatry. A Pained Filth retreats like any unit. Filth that stays on the map after Abhoth's death keeps standing where it is, still its old owner's units, and counts for the Combat of the next Abhoth and for The Brood.*

**Requirement:** choose one: either your faction has **4+ different Monster types** in play, Filth Tokens included, **or** **8+ Monsters** in all, Filth Tokens included. *(Met by itself the moment it is true.)*

**The Brood** (Ongoing): Gates in areas containing Filth Tokens do not count during the Doom phase. Does not apply to Abhoth's faction. *In Bingle: those Gates score no Doom in the Doom phase and none for a Ritual (they still give Power in Gather Power). The Doom phase log says which Gates were spoiled.*

**Strategy (from the wiki).**
- *Any:* with any faction Abhoth can cause a lot of problems for your enemies.
- *Black Goat:* use Filth to safely and easily get her spellbook requirements. Yellow Sign can use it to hold down Desecrated areas. All factions can use them to defend their own Gates and seed areas with meat shields for an assault. The Brood puts your foes on their heels: they must spend resources clearing out Filth from their areas, lead an assault against Abhoth (who may himself be fortified behind a wall of Filth Tokens), or lose Doom points!
- *Sleeper:* using Lethargy you can save a lot of Power until the end of the round, after everyone else is out. Using Abhoth at this time is devastating: you can easily cripple an opponent's Gather Power and Doom phase with The Brood.
- *Yellow Sign:* place Filth Tokens on all of your abandoned Desecration markers.

*The bots* put Filth where Cultists or a Gate stand unguarded (best where an enemy threatens), and do not spend it elsewhere.

### Chaugnar Faugn

![](piece:chaugnar-faugn:green) *The horror from the hills, a great old one who molds human flesh like clay. He rules his sub-human worshipers from his throne below.*

**Awaken:** 1. Your controlled Gate is in an area with your Great Old One. 2. Pay 4 Power, and place Chaugnar Faugn in the area containing the Gate. **Combat:** 3.

**Miri Nigri** (Ongoing): in a battle taking place in an area with your controlled Gate, add **+3 Combat dice**. *(Yours as long as he is on the map; the area need not be his.)*

**Requirement:** as your Action for a round, **discard 2 Elder Signs** (you receive no Doom for them). *In Bingle the button *Discard 2 Elder Signs* offers the pairs of values you hold; the signs go back to the pool.*

**Curse of Chaugnar Faugn** (Ongoing): turn all Elder Signs in the pool face-up. When any player receives an Elder Sign, **you pick which one they receive**. If you lose this spellbook, turn the Elder Signs face-down again, and mix them up. *In Bingle the bag's contents (how many 1s, 2s and 3s) are shown to everybody while the Curse is on, and whenever a player receives an Elder Sign — yours too — you are asked at once which value it has; when only one value is left there is nothing to ask.*

**Strategy (from the wiki).**
- *Any:* grab Chaugnar Faugn to boost your Gate defenses, and grab his spellbook to gain leverage over your foes: if they are good, you may give them 2s, but if they are bad, you give them only 1s (of course keeping the 3s for yourself!).
- *Black Goat:* Miri Nigri makes your fortified areas even more difficult to uproot. The Curse pairs nicely with Blood Sacrifice as well.
- *Crawling Chaos:* Flying Polyps can make invisible the enemy forces that seek to harm Chaugnar. In the meantime, use the Curse to gain 6 Doom points in Elder Signs for each successful Harbinger attack. Attacking an area with 2 enemy Great Old Ones with this combo can win the game instantly.
- *Great Cthulhu:* your precious few Gates can now better defend themselves while Cthulhu's army tears down the competition. Get a guaranteed 3 Doom when you bring big green back.
- *Tcho-Tcho:* with 3 combat dice from Miri Nigri your Proto-Shoggoths are freed up to take your opponent's dice away with Terror. With 3 High Priests in the Doom phase you can sacrifice them all to net 9 Doom with the Curse.
- *Windwalker:* with 2 faction Great Old Ones in place you can gain a guaranteed 6 Doom with Rituals locked in at 5 Power with the Herald of the Outer Gods spellbook! That is a steal.
- *Yellow Sign:* keep Chaugnar safe with Hastur. Any attempt on his life will result in catastrophic losses on your attacker's army. A good round of Desecrations will result in a massive Doom gain.

*The bots* awaken him early (a fortress at their Gates), discard their two cheapest Elder Signs for the Curse, and — when they hold it — give themselves 3s and others 1s.

### Cthugha

![](piece:cthugha:green) *The Great Old One of Fire, whose consuming flames cannot be quenched. Serve him!*

**Awaken:** 1. Your controlled Gate is in an area with your Great Old One. 2. Pay Power equal to **6 minus your Great Old One's awakening cost**; if the result is negative, you *gain* that much Power. 3. **Replace your Great Old One with Cthugha.** *In Bingle the awakening is one button, then the area and — when several of your Great Old Ones stand there — the one he replaces (clicked as a unit). The cost is the one that Great Old One has now: Cthulhu 10 the first time and 4 after (Immortal), Nyarlathotep 10, Shub-Niggurath 8, an Independent 4 or 6… The replaced figure goes back to the pool (an Independent's card and spellbook with it) and can be awakened again later.* **Combat:** equals the Combat of an enemy Great Old One in the battle (your choice — the best is taken); if there is none, 0.

**Fire Vampires** (Post-Battle): if Cthugha is involved in a battle, after all Kill results are assigned but before they are applied, you may choose to **spare** one or more Killed enemy units by reducing their Kill to a Pain instead. For each enemy unit you spare in this way, you gain 1 Power. You cannot use Fire Vampires (or Firestorm) against an enemy with zero Power (or a hibernating Windwalker). *In Bingle the units that took a Kill stay on the map until you have chosen: click each one you spare, or press Done to let the rest die. A spared unit is Pained and retreats.*

**Requirement:** kill an enemy Great Old One in battle. **Firestorm** (Post-Battle): if Cthugha is involved in a battle, for each Killed enemy unit you spare you also gain **1 Elder Sign**.

**Strategy (from the wiki).**
- *Crawling Chaos:* spare enemy Great Old Ones with Fire Vampires, then pick up two Elder Signs with Nyarlathotep's Harbinger. For maximum effect, pick a faction that is out of Power and isolate the Great Old One with Madness for repeat attacks. With Firestorm you are looking at 3 Elder Signs per battle! Imagine fighting two enemy Great Old Ones at once with this setup!
- *Bokrug / King in Yellow combo:* if another player has a Great Old One with 0 combat dice, like Bokrug or the King in Yellow, isolate him with Madness and attack with Nyarlathotep and Cthugha as a pair. (Watch out for a Dread Curse-enabled Opener player and Unlimited Battle armies. Keep your Hunting Horrors ready for defense.)
- *Great Cthulhu:* it is beneficial to re-summon Cthulhu, so for 6 Power (2 for Cthugha, 4 for Cthulhu) you get 2 Elder Signs. If you can Submerge to surprise-attack an enemy Great Old One (and kill it), you can start raking in the Elder Signs with Firestorm.
- *Tcho-Tcho:* with Ubbo-Sathla re-summoning automatically every Doom phase, Cthugha is free! Go after an enemy Great Old One to get Cthugha's spellbook *and* your faction spellbook that requires a Great Old One to die.

*The bots* replace Cthulhu (when he has awakened before) or Ubbo-Sathla, as the two combos above say, and otherwise leave him alone; they spare enemy Great Old Ones (and every unit once they hold Firestorm).

### Mother Hydra

![](piece:mother-hydra:green) *The ultimate development of the Deep One ruling class, Mother Hydra is a title that rotates, given to the current most-powerful deep one female.*

**Awaken:** 1. Your controlled Gate is in an area with your Great Old One. 2. Pay 4 Power, and place Mother Hydra in the area containing the Gate. **Combat:** 6 minus the number of enemy units in the area (minimum 1).

**The Agony Sting** (Action: Cost 1): choose any Ocean area. All enemy Cultists in that area must be moved into adjacent land areas by their owners (your Cultists are immune). In a dispute over who moves first, you decide. *In Bingle each owner, in turn order from you, clicks a Cultist and a land area next to the ocean until the ocean is empty; a Gate whose holder leaves is abandoned.*

**Requirement:** choose one: **either** you control no Great Old Ones in ocean areas, **or** enemy factions control no Great Old Ones in ocean areas. *(Great Old Ones of the cult and Independents count; Cthulhu submerged is in an ocean. Met by itself as soon as it is true — Mother Hydra herself counts if she stands in an ocean.)* **The Zygote** (Action: Cost 1): take all Acolyte Cultists in your pool and place them on the map in any areas in which you have a unit. *In Bingle: press the button, then click an area for each Acolyte.*

**Strategy (from the wiki).**
- *Any:* **Neutral spellbook combo with The Mao Ceremony** — sacrifice every Cultist for 6 Power, and get them all back with Mother Hydra for 1. Make sure you go first, otherwise your Gates will be open for capture until your turn comes around.
- *Black Goat:* use the Zygote to replace Cultists from awakening Shub, spellbook requirements and Blood Sacrifice. Use the Agony Sting to harass various ocean Gates on the cheap.
- *Great Cthulhu:* have extra Cultists? Absorb every last one for +3 dice each, then summon them all back for 1 Power with the Zygote. Devolve all the Cultists hanging out in R'lyeh to combat intruders, then summon all the Cultists back when the coast is clear. *With Undimensioned:* maximize the Devolve/Absorb combos to make sure your units are in the right places at the right times.
- *Opener of the Way:* can make great use of the Zygote to replace upgraded units.
- *Sleeper:* save your Power using Lethargy, then empty ocean areas you wish to capture. Burrow lets you cheaply move your forces into position.
- *Tcho-Tcho:* you have the unique ability (aside from the Sleeper) to have a lot of Power after everyone else is out, when you sacrifice your High Priests. Use this Power to empty Gates with the Agony Sting and move your own units in to capture.

*The bots* use the Zygote whenever two or more Acolytes wait in the pool and sting oceans only where it drives out an enemy's Gate-holder or several Cultists.

### Yig

![](piece:yig:green) *The rattlesnake god, father of serpents. Is he the true terror behind the myths of Set and Quetzalcoatl?*

**Awaken:** 1. Your controlled Gate is in an area with your Great Old One. 2. Pay 4 Power, and place Yig in the area containing the Gate. **Combat:** 2.

**Snakebite** (Post-Battle): your Cultists are now poisonous. If any of your Cultists are Killed in battle, the enemy receives 1 extra Kill result. (He only takes 1 extra Kill regardless of how many Cultists die.) *In Bingle: Yig must be on the map (anywhere); after the results of a battle are assigned, the cult that killed any of your Cultists assigns one more Kill to its own units.*

**Requirement:** as your Action for a round, **remove one of your controlled Gates from the map**. **Messenger of Yig** (Ongoing): in the Doom phase, each other player must decide if they will donate 1 Power from their total to you. For each player that refuses, gain 1 Doom point. *In Bingle every other cult is asked after the Gates have scored; a cult with no Power cannot give, so it is counted as refusing.*

**Strategy (from the wiki).** Use Yig to bolster your defenses, and then leverage his ability to gain extra Power and/or Doom!

*The bots* wake him for defense (poisoned Cultists make a Gate costly to take), give a Gate up for the spellbook only when they have five, and give 1 Power to Yig's controller when they can spare 3.

## The bots

The bots awaken the Independents when they have the Power to spare (Chaugnar Faugn first, then Mother Hydra, Yig and Abhoth; Cthugha only to replace Cthulhu or Ubbo-Sathla), and play the abilities as described under each. They do not decline a free spellbook, and they never abandon a Gate for Yig's requirement unless they hold plenty.
