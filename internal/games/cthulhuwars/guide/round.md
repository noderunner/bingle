# The round

A game is a series of rounds. Each round has four steps, always in this order.

> [!NOTE]
> **The first round is special.** Everyone starts with 8 Power, so the game begins at the third step:
> *who plays first* is not decided by Power. **Great Cthulhu is always the first player of the game, wherever it
> sits at the table.** At a table without Great Cthulhu the first player is the cult **first by name** — the rule
> book's own suggestion — in the order *Black Goat, Crawling Chaos, Great Cthulhu, Sleeper, Windwalker, Yellow Sign*
> (so Black Goat comes before Yellow Sign, and the Sleeper before the Windwalker). The first player chooses the
> direction of play — clockwise or counter-clockwise — for the whole round, the first Action phase included, and the
> **Doom phase is skipped** in that first round. (The Windwalker, when it is at the table, still begins first of all:
> it chooses where to set up, and then the first player chooses the direction.) From the second round on, the first
> player is the cult with the most Power, as below.

## 1. Action phase

Starting with the **first player** and going in the chosen direction (clockwise or counter-clockwise), each
player in turn does **one** of:

- spend Power on an [action](tab:actions), or
- **pass** and drop to **0 Power** for the rest of the phase.

After an action the turn moves on. The phase ends when no player has Power left.

> [!TIP]
> **Unlimited actions** cost nothing and do not end your turn: *controlling* a Gate, revealing your Elder Signs, Black
> Goat's summoning and some spellbook powers. Do them as often as you like, then take your action. After your action
> the turn ends by itself unless a free action worth waiting for is left.

A **Windwalker** may *Hibernate* instead of acting: it gains Power now, takes no more actions this phase (it is skipped
as though it were at 0 Power) and keeps the Power it banked: in Gather Power it is added to what it gathers. See
[the Windwalker](tab:factions).

## 2. Gather Power

Each cult gains Power:

| Source | Power |
|---|---|
| Each of your Cultists on the map (a High Priest is one) | 1 |
| Each Gate you control | 2 |
| Each abandoned Gate (nobody controls it) | 1 — to **every** player |
| Each of your Cultists that was *captured* and goes home | 1 to the captor |
| Yellow Sign: each Desecrated area where you have a unit | 1 (*Feast*) |
| Great Cthulhu: each enemy Gate in an ocean (*Y'ha-Nthlei*) | 1 |
| Black Goat: each Dark Young, with *The Red Sign* | 1 |
| Sleeper: each Monster Tsathoggua captured, which is sacrificed | 1 |

Power left over from the Action phase is kept, and what is gathered is added to it. Every cult spends or drops all of
its Power during the Action phase — except a **Windwalker** that hibernated, which brings the Power it banked. A
**Sleeper**'s sleeping Gate and Cultist (*Cursed Slumber*) still count for the Gate's 2 Power and the Cultist's 1.
The game log says where each cult's Power came from.

Anyone below **half of the leader's Power (rounded up)** is raised to that number. Captured Cultists then return
to their owners' pools.

A player who holds a **High Priest** may sacrifice him now (*Unspeakable Oath*) for 2 Power — which is how a cult
that trails the leader can become the first player.

## 3. Determine first player

The cult with the **most Power** is first next round and chooses the direction of play. On a tie, the player who
was first this round chooses among the tied cults. (In the first round of the game it is not Power but the table:
Great Cthulhu, or the first cult by name — see the note above.)

The cults sit in the order of the seats at the table — the player list of the game page follows it. **Clockwise**
passes play *down* that list and from the last cult back to the first; **counter-clockwise** passes it *up* the list.
The two choices are spelled out as who plays after whom, and the bar at the top of the page keeps showing the order
in force.

Being the first player meets one of the Windwalker's requirements.

## 4. Doom phase

First every Gate you control scores **1 Doom** (a Gate that sleeps on the Sleeper's faction card counts too). The
game log gives, for each cult, the Doom track before and after and what the total is made of — *Great Cthulhu scores
3 Doom for 3 Gates: 3 → 6 on the Doom track (3 from Gates, 3 from gifts)* — and the cult's Doom counter says the same
when you hover it. Doom also comes from Rituals, from Yellow Sign's gift of 3 Doom to another cult, and from revealed
Elder Signs.

Then, in turn order from the first player, every cult in turn may:

- **perform the Ritual of Annihilation** (once per Doom phase),
- **reveal** its Elder Signs,
- **buy a Loyalty Card** — one in a Doom phase, a neutral Monster's for 2 Doom or a Terror's for 2 Doom and 2 Power;
  you may still perform the Ritual (see [Neutral](tab:neutral)), or
- use a Doom-phase ability: Black Goat's *Blood Sacrifice*, the Sleeper's *Death from Below*, the Wamps'
  *Crypt Dweller*, any cult's *Unspeakable Oath* when the High Priests are in play.

Some cards act on their own at the start of the Doom phase, after the Gates have scored: every **Moonbeast** leaves the
spellbook it lay on and comes to a Gate of its owner, **Gnorri** (two or three in play) earn their extra Doom, the
**Brood** of Abhoth spoils the Gates beside Filth, and **Messenger of Yig** asks every other cult for 1 Power.

A Sleeper that used *Ancient Sorcery* gets its Serpent Man back at the **end** of the Doom phase, with 1 Power.

### The Ritual of Annihilation

Pay the current cost to gain **1 Doom per Gate** you control and **1 Elder Sign per Great Old One** you have in
play, and the marker on the **Ritual of Annihilation track** (above the map) moves one space. The cost is the number
on the space the marker stands on, so it goes up as Rituals are performed; **how many spaces the track has
depends on the number of players**:

| Players | Spaces and their cost in Power |
|---|---|
| 3 | 5 · 6 · 7 · 8 · 9 · 10 |
| 4 | 5 · 6 · 7 · 7 · 8 · 8 · 9 · 10 |
| 5 | 5 · 6 · 6 · 7 · 7 · 8 · 8 · 9 · 9 · 10 |

A **Windwalker** with *Herald of the Outer Gods* always pays **5**, whatever the track says (the marker still moves).

After the last space comes **Instant Death**: when the marker gets there the game ends once that Doom phase has
been scored (if something other than a Ritual moved it in another phase, at the next Doom phase). Players who have
not had their chance yet may still perform one more Ritual, at 10 Power each.

### Elder Signs

Elder Signs are worth **1, 2 or 3 Doom** each (a hidden bag holds 18 ones, 12 twos and 6 threes). You may look at
your own at any time — they are shown in your cult's panel and nobody else can see them; the others see only how
many you hold. The number still in the bag is public.

**Revealing** turns *all* the Elder Signs you hold face up at once: their total is added to your Doom, and their
values become public. The revealed signs stay on view next to your score. Signs you gain later stay hidden until you
reveal again. Every sign still hidden is revealed when the game ends.

## Ending the game

The game ends at the end of a Doom phase in which

- someone has reached **30 Doom**, or
- the Ritual marker reached **Instant Death** at the end of the track.

Then every cult reveals its Elder Signs. Among the cults with **all six spellbooks**, the one with the most Doom wins.
If **nobody** has six, humanity survives and every cult loses.

> [!WARNING]
> Doom without spellbooks is worth nothing. A cult with 40 Doom and five books loses to a cult with 25 Doom and six.
