# Actions

On your turn you take **one action** and pay its cost in Power. Some things are *unlimited* and free.

## The main actions

| Action | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Recruit** | 1 (3 for a High Priest) | Place a Cultist from your pool in an area where you already have a unit. |
| **Summon** | monster's cost | Place a monster from your pool in an area with a Gate **you control**. |
| **Awaken** | GOO's cost | Place a Great Old One in the area its card allows. See below. |
| **Build a Gate** | 3 | A Cultist in an area with **no** Gate builds one there and controls it. |
| **Move** | 1 per unit | Move your units one area each (once each) to an adjacent area. |
| **Battle** | 1 | Fight one enemy cult in an area where you both have units. See [Battle](tab:battle). |
| **Capture** | 1 | A monster or Great Old One takes an unprotected enemy Cultist. |
| **Pass** | 0 | Drop your Power to 0; you are out of the Action phase. |

> [!NOTE]
> With **all six spellbooks** you may Battle as an *unlimited* action, once per area per phase.

### Recruit

You may recruit only where you have a unit already — or anywhere if you have no units on the map.
With the **High Priests** in the game, the *High Priest* can be recruited the same way for 3 Power (once he is in your
pool): he is a Cultist for every purpose, gives 1 Power in Gather Power, can build and hold a Gate and can be captured.

### Summon

Every monster you can summon has a button of its own, with the Power it costs just now on it (the cost can change: a
Windwalker's Gnoph-Keh, Black Goat's *Thousand Young*); hover it for the unit's card — its numbers, abilities and the
spellbooks that bear on it. Press it, then choose one of your Gates. **Black Goat** can summon as an unlimited action.
The Windwalker's **Gnoph-Keh** costs as many Power as there are Gnoph-Keh in your pool (4, then 3, 2, 1).

### Awaken

Every Great Old One you can awaken just now has a button of its own — *Awaken Cthulhu*, with the Power it costs on it;
hover it for the unit's card, with the steps below. Press it, then choose the area. Awakening is an Action: what must
stand on the map, what it costs, and where the Great Old One appears.

| Great Old One | How to awaken |
|---|---|
| ![](piece:cthulhu:green) **Cthulhu** | **1.** There must be a Gate in your **starting area** (the South Pacific) — yours, abandoned or an enemy's. **2.** Pay **10** Power — **4** every time after the first (Immortal). **3.** Cthulhu appears in your starting area, and you gain 1 Elder Sign. |
| ![](piece:nyarlathotep:blue) **Nyarlathotep** | **1.** You must control a Gate. **2.** Pay **10** Power. **3.** Nyarlathotep appears at that Gate. |
| ![](piece:shub-niggurath:red) **Shub-Niggurath** | **1.** You must control a Gate and have at least **2 Cultists** on the map, in any areas. **2.** Pay **8** Power. **3.** Eliminate 2 of your Cultists (of your choice; they return to your pool); Shub-Niggurath appears at that Gate. |
| ![](piece:king-in-yellow:yellow) **King in Yellow** | **1.** You must have a unit in an area that has **no Gate**. **2.** Pay **4** Power. **3.** The King appears in that area. |
| ![](piece:hastur:yellow) **Hastur** | **1.** You must control a Gate in an area where the King in Yellow stands. **2.** Pay **10** Power. **3.** Hastur appears in that area. |
| ![](piece:rhan-tegoth:white) **Rhan-Tegoth** | **1.** Pay **6** Power. **2.** Rhan-Tegoth appears in an area marked with the Windwalker sigil. |
| ![](piece:ithaqua:white) **Ithaqua** | **1.** Rhan-Tegoth has been awakened (he need not be in play). **2.** A Gate must stand in an area marked with the Windwalker sigil — anyone's: you need not control it. **3.** Pay **6** Power: Ithaqua replaces that Gate. |
| ![](piece:tsathoggua:orange) **Tsathoggua** | **1.** You must have a Formless Spawn on the map. **2.** Pay **8** Power. **3.** Tsathoggua appears in the area of a Formless Spawn. |

An **Independent Great Old One** has the same button, *Awaken Abhoth (4)*, whenever you control a Gate in an area where
you have a Great Old One of your own (see [Independents](tab:independents)); Cthugha asks which Great Old One he
replaces.

Awakening a Great Old One is also what meets that cult's *Awaken* requirement, and Great Cthulhu gains 1 Elder Sign
whenever *any* Great Old One of his cult wakes (Immortal). A Great Old One that is killed returns to the pool and can
be awakened again at the same price (Cthulhu's 4 Power after the first time included).

### Build a Gate

The button says what it costs (*Build Gate (3)*) and its card the rule: a Cultist — the High Priest too, and with
The Red Sign a Dark Young — builds a Gate in the area it stands in, which must have none yet. There is room for **one
Gate in an area**, on land or in an ocean. You control it at once, and the unit that built it stands on it. The
Gate's controller needs a unit that can hold it in the area to keep it.

### Move

Click a unit that glows, then its destination, and repeat for further units; press *Done* when finished. Each
unit may move **once** per action and costs 1 Power.

**Gates and the units on them.** Every Gate you control is *occupied* by one of your units — an Acolyte, the High
Priest or a Dark Young — which is drawn **on the Gate**, apart from the others of its kind in the area: with 6 Acolytes
in an area one stands on the Gate and a stack of 5 beside it.

- **Giving a Gate up** is moving the unit that stands on it: click the figure on the Gate, then the area you send it
  to — a Move step like any other, **1 Power** — or **the Gate itself**, which steps it off where it stands: that is
  **free**, uses no action and needs no confirming (once per Gate in a turn). Either way the Gate is **abandoned**
  whatever else stands there — it gives 1 Power to every player in Gather Power, scores you no Doom and the first
  Cultist of anyone's to arrive takes it. Moving one of the units *beside* the Gate never touches it.
- **Putting a unit on a Gate** is free and does not end your turn — also in the middle of a Move action. While it is
  your turn, select one of the units of the area that can hold a Gate (not the one on it), then click the Gate: it
  takes control of an abandoned Gate, or takes the place of the unit on your own (a Gate changes hands once in a turn).
  A Cultist that **arrives** at an abandoned Gate takes control of it at once — nobody ever declines — unless you gave
  the Gate up this turn.
- If the unit on a Gate is **killed** against your will, another of your units that can hold the Gate steps onto it;
  the Gate is abandoned only when you have none left. A unit **captured** off its Gate is different: see Capture.

**Crawling Chaos** can move up to **2 areas** (Flight).

> [!TIP]
> Moving a single unit and ending the turn keeps Power in reserve to answer what the others do.

**Ice Age.** An action of an enemy that *ends* in the area of the Windwalker's Ice Age token costs 1 Power more —
recruiting, summoning, awakening, building a Gate, a unit that moves in, a Battle, a Capture. A Move is charged once.

**Arctic Wind.** When Ithaqua moves (and his owner holds the spellbook), the Windwalker's other units that stand
with him may follow him to the same area for nothing.

**Burrow.** After a Move in which the Sleeper spent 2 or more Power it regains 1 Power.

### Capture

Your Great Old One may capture an enemy Cultist in its area if the enemy has no monster or Great Old One of equal
rank there. A monster may capture a Cultist if the enemy has no monster or GOO there.
A captured Cultist is held by you and goes home during **Gather Power**, giving you 1 Power.

**Which Cultist.** When the enemy has several Cultists in the area and one of them holds a Gate, you choose: one from
the stack beside the Gate, or the Cultist on the Gate ("Acolyte on the Gate"). When it is the only one (or the High
Priest holds the Gate), no choice is asked. Capturing the unit that holds a Gate **leaves the Gate abandoned**, even
if the enemy has other Cultists there, and a Cultist (or High Priest) of your own in the area **steps onto it at once**.
If you have none there it stays abandoned and open to whoever puts a unit on it first; its old owner can do so in
its own turn with the free "put a unit on the Gate" action.

**Ferox.** While Ithaqua is in play, the Windwalker's Cultists cannot be captured by Monsters — only by Great Old Ones.

**Capture Monster.** The Sleeper's Tsathoggua, with that spellbook, may capture an enemy *Monster* in the same way.
The monster is held and is sacrificed in the next Gather Power for 1 Power.

## Unlimited actions (free)

- **Put a unit on a Gate** of the area it stands in — an abandoned Gate you take control of, or your own whose unit you replace (this happens by itself when a Cultist arrives at an abandoned Gate).
- **Reveal** your Elder Signs (all of them: their total is added to your Doom and they become public).
- Black Goat: **Summon** monsters.
- Windwalker: **Take the free spellbook** of its fifth requirement — at any time (it comes with an Elder Sign for each enemy that has all six spellbooks, at most 3, so it pays to wait).
- With the High Priests: **Unspeakable Oath** — sacrifice your High Priest for 2 Power (see below).
- **Pay 1 Doom to free a spellbook** from a Moonbeast (no Action, no Power).
- Spellbook actions marked *Unlimited* (Great Cthulhu's *Devolve* may also be used as a reaction, below).

### The abilities of the Great Old Ones

Every Great Old One has an ability of its own on its card. The unit's card (hover its icon, or click it on the map)
lists it, with how to awaken it.

| Great Old One | Ability | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![](piece:cthulhu:green) Cthulhu | **Devour** | Pre-Battle | The enemy player chooses and eliminates one of his Monsters or Cultists in the battle. |
| ![](piece:nyarlathotep:blue) Nyarlathotep | **The Harbinger** | Post-Battle | If he is in a battle in which enemy Great Old Ones are Pained or Killed, you receive Power equal to half the cost of awakening them — or 2 Elder Signs for each. |
| ![](piece:shub-niggurath:red) Shub-Niggurath | **Avatar** | Action, cost 1 | Choose an area and a faction: swap Shub-Niggurath with a Monster or Cultist of that faction there (its owner chooses which). |
| ![](piece:king-in-yellow:yellow) King in Yellow | **Desecrate** | Action, cost 2 | With no Desecration token in his area, roll 1 die: a result no higher than the number of your units there (the King included) places a token. Whether you succeed or fail, place a Monster or Cultist with a cost of 2 or less there. |
| ![](piece:hastur:yellow) Hastur | **Vengeance** | Post-Battle | If he is in a battle, you choose which combat results are applied to which enemy units. |
| ![](piece:rhan-tegoth:white) Rhan-Tegoth | **Eternal** | Post-Battle | If he receives a Pain or a Kill, you may pay 1 Power to cancel it; he can only receive one result per battle. |
| ![](piece:ithaqua:white) Ithaqua | **Ferox** | Ongoing | While he is in play your Cultists cannot be captured by enemy Monsters or Terrors (Great Old Ones still can). |
| ![](piece:tsathoggua:orange) Tsathoggua | **Lethargy** | Action, cost 0 | While he is in play, do nothing: it counts as an Action. |

**Desecrate** in play: the button rolls the die at once — the result is final and cannot be taken back — and the Monster
or Cultist is chosen right after it. The King acts on the area he stands in; an area holds one token; the units counted
are all of yours there, the King included, but not the one the action places.

### Hibernate (Windwalker)

Cost 0. Gain 1 Power for each enemy Great Old One in play — at most as much as you already have. From then on
you take no more actions in this Action phase, as though you were at 0 Power, and the Power you have is kept: in
Gather Power it is added to what you gather.

### Lethargy (Sleeper)

Cost 0. While Tsathoggua is in play, do nothing: it counts as an Action. The game offers it only while another cult
still has Power to spend — otherwise it would only delay the end of the phase.

### Sleeper requirements

Three of the Sleeper's requirements are actions of their own, each costing 3 Power: **Boon** (every other player
gains 1 Power), **Bestow** (one other player of your choice gains 3 Power) and **Bane** (every other player loses 1
Power; nobody goes below 0). The fourth is to roll 6 dice in one battle, the fifth to perform a Ritual and the
sixth to awaken Tsathoggua.

### Reactions

A few abilities may be used on **another player's turn**: Great Cthulhu's *Devolve* (after any player's action),
Black Goat's *Necrophagy*, Crawling Chaos' *Madness* and the Windwalker's *Cannibalism* (in battles they are not
part of), the Sleeper's *Energy Nexus* (just before a battle in an area with a Wizard) and, with the High Priests,
every cult's *Unspeakable Oath*. After another player's
action the game asks the holder whether to use it — the table waits for the answer — and when two players want one,
the first player goes first. So that a game is never held up, **your reactions are passed for you by default** and you
choose when you would like to be asked instead, in the **reactions** of the game options — they are there from the
first move, before the spellbook is earned:

- **When I'm targeted** (on by default; the **?** next to it explains it): you are asked when an opponent's *action*
  is aimed at you — at you as a player (The Thousand Forms, Ghroth, a gift of Doom or Power, a battle or a capture
  against you), at one of your units, or at an area where you have a presence (any unit of yours, or a Gate you
  control: an enemy that moves into it, recruits or summons in it, builds a Gate or awakens a Great Old One there,
  or brings Cthulhu up out of the sea there). In a battle you are not part of, the same goes for the area it is
  fought in. Effects that happen *as part of a battle* — Madness moving a unit to an area its player chooses,
  retreats, kills and pains — are not actions and never ask you.
- **After any other player's action**: you are asked every time.
- **After chosen players' actions**: you are asked after the actions of the players you tick.

Change it at any time, also while the other players are acting. When the game does ask you, the question also offers
*Stop being asked* for the reason that made it ask. Computer players and AI agents are asked every time.

### Unspeakable Oath (High Priests)

At the end of any player's Action — even when it is not your turn — and also in Gather Power and in the Doom phase,
you may sacrifice your High Priest: he returns to your pool and you gain **2 Power**. **You are asked after every
action of the others** (and in Gather Power) when your reactions settings say so — by default only when you are
targeted; otherwise it is passed for you. In the Doom phase it is offered when 2 Power more
would pay for a Ritual. You can always take the Oath on your own turn as an unlimited action. A sacrificed High
Priest can be recruited again for 3 Power.
