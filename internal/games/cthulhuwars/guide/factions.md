# The eight cults

Each cult has its own units, a faction ability, six spellbooks it can earn and six requirements that earn them. Click a faction name in the table of contents to jump to it. Each cult's **glyph** — its sigil — is drawn under its name; the map shows it where the cult begins.
The first four are the core set; the **Windwalker** and the **Sleeper** come from the first expansion, the **Opener of the Way** and the **Tcho-Tcho** from the next ones, and the
**High Priests** are an option for every cult (see the end of this page).

### Cthulhu: awakening and ability

1. There must be a **Gate in your starting area**, the South Pacific — yours, abandoned or an enemy's.
2. Pay **10** Power; **4** Power every time after the first (Immortal).
3. Cthulhu appears in the South Pacific, and you gain 1 Elder Sign.

**Devour** (Pre-Battle): the enemy player chooses and eliminates one of his Monsters or Cultists in the battle.

### Playing it

- Build Gates in the **oceans**: they feed *Y'ha-Nthlei* and two of your requirements, and oceans are hard for others to reach. Cthulhu wakes only in the South Pacific, so keep a Gate there.
- Cthulhu's **Devour** makes the enemy eliminate a unit every time he fights — it also pays the *Kill* requirement.
- Keep Cultists alive to hold Gates; Deep Ones are cheap bodies to protect them.

### Nyarlathotep: awakening and ability

1. You must **control a Gate**.
2. Pay **10** Power.
3. Nyarlathotep appears at that Gate.

**The Harbinger** (Post-Battle): if Nyarlathotep is in a battle in which one or more enemy Great Old Ones are Pained or
Killed, you receive Power equal to half the cost of awakening those Great Old Ones. For each enemy Great Old One
Pained or Killed you may choose 2 Elder Signs instead of the Power.

### Playing it

- **Flight** means nobody's Cultists are safe: strike two areas away and capture.
- Nyarlathotep's strength grows with **every spellbook on every faction card** — he gets scarier as the game goes on.
- Spend Power on the *pay 4 / pay 6* requirements early: they are the cheapest spellbooks in the game.

## Black Goat

![Black Goat glyph|96](guide/glyphs/bg.svg "Black Goat's glyph — its sigil: shown where the cult begins on the map.")

**Start:** South America · **Ability:** Fertility Cult: summoning monsters is an Unlimited action for you.

> [!TIP]
> Fertility Cult lets you summon as many Monsters in one turn as you can pay for — but a summon is also an action
> of its own: after a single Monster (a Ghoul for 1 Power is the classic way of marking time) you can press **Done**
> and end your turn, keeping the rest of your Power. *Pass* is for when you take no action at all, and it drops your
> Power to 0.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](piece:cultist:red) | **Acolyte Cultist** | Cultist | 6 | 1 (recruit) | 0 |  |
| ![](piece:ghoul:red) | **Ghoul** | Monster | 2 | 1 | 0 | Necrophagy (spellbook): drawn into any battle. |
| ![](piece:fungi:red) | **Fungi** | Monster | 4 | 2 | 1 | Ghroth (spellbook) counts the areas that hold Fungi. |
| ![](piece:dark-young:red) | **Dark Young** | Monster | 3 | 3 | 2 | The Red Sign (spellbook): can create and control Gates. |
| ![](piece:shub-niggurath:red) | **Shub-Niggurath** | Great Old One | 1 | 8 | see text | Combat equals your controlled Gates plus your Cultists in play (+1 per Dark Young with The Red Sign). Awaken for 8 Power at a Gate you control by eliminating 2 of your Cultists. Avatar (action, 1 Power): swap places with a Monster or Cultist of a faction in another area. |

### Shub-Niggurath: awakening and ability

1. You must **control a Gate** and have at least **2 Cultists** on the map — in any areas.
2. Pay **8** Power.
3. Eliminate 2 of your Cultists (they return to your pool); Shub-Niggurath appears at that Gate.

**Avatar** (Action, cost 1): choose an area and a faction. Swap the location of Shub-Niggurath with that of a Monster or
Cultist in the chosen area; the owner of the chosen faction chooses which unit to relocate.

### Playing it

- **Summon for free** (as an unlimited action) the moment you have Power and a Gate.
- Spread out: three of your requirements are *units in 4, 6, 8 areas*.
- **Frenzy** makes your Cultists dangerous; Shub-Niggurath grows with every Gate and Cultist you have.

## Yellow Sign

![Yellow Sign glyph|96](guide/glyphs/ys.svg "Yellow Sign's glyph — its sigil: shown where the cult begins on the map.")

**Start:** Europe · **Ability:** Feast: in Gather Power gain 1 Power for each area that holds a Desecration token and at least one of your units.

| | Unit | Class | Pieces | Cost | Combat | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](piece:cultist:yellow) | **Acolyte Cultist** | Cultist | 6 | 1 (recruit) | 0 |  |
| ![](piece:undead:yellow) | **Undead** | Monster | 6 | 1 | see text | Combat: roll one die fewer than the number of Undead in the battle. |
| ![](piece:byakhee:yellow) | **Byakhee** | Monster | 4 | 2 | see text | Combat: roll one die more than the number of Byakhee in the battle. |
| ![](piece:king-in-yellow:yellow) | **King in Yellow** | Great Old One | 1 | 4 | 0 | Desecrate (action, cost 2): roll against your units in his area; whatever the roll, place a Monster or Cultist of cost 2 or less there. |
| ![](piece:hastur:yellow) | **Hastur** | Great Old One | 1 | 10 | see text | Combat equals the current cost of a Ritual of Annihilation. Vengeance (post-battle): you choose which combat results are applied to which enemy units. |

### The King in Yellow and Hastur: awakening and abilities

**King in Yellow:** 1. You must have a unit in an area that has **no Gate**. 2. Pay **4** Power. 3. He appears in that area.

**Desecrate** (Action, cost 2 — 1 with The Third Eye while Hastur is in play): if the King is in an area with no
Desecration token, roll 1 die. If the roll is equal to or less than the number of your units in the area (the King
included), place a Desecration token there. **Whether you succeed or fail**, place a Monster or Cultist with a cost of
2 or less (an Acolyte, an Undead or a Byakhee) from your pool in the area — you choose which, after the roll. A token
on a glyph area meets that requirement; every token is a Power in Gather Power for each area you share with it (*Feast*).

**Hastur:** 1. You must control a Gate in an area where the King in Yellow stands. 2. Pay **10** Power. 3. Hastur
appears in that area.

**Vengeance** (Post-Battle): if Hastur is involved in a battle, you choose which combat results are applied to which
enemy units (for instance, a Kill to a particular Great Old One).

### Playing it

- The **King in Yellow** needs no Gate — awaken him anywhere you have a unit for only 4 Power.
- **Desecrate** a dragon, a chevron and a thorns area for three of your six spellbooks.
- **Feast**: stay in Desecrated areas and your Power income grows.

## Windwalker

![Windwalker glyph|96](guide/glyphs/ww.svg "Windwalker's glyph — its sigil: shown where the cult begins on the map.")

**Start:** one of the two areas marked with its sigil (the Arctic Ocean or Antarctica), chosen by its player at the very
start of the game · **Ability:** Hibernate (Action, cost 0): gain 1 Power for each enemy Great Old One in play — at most as much as you already have; you take no more actions this Action phase and the Power you have is kept and added to what you gather in Gather Power.

| | Unit | Class | Pieces | Cost | Combat | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](piece:cultist:white) | **Acolyte Cultist** | Cultist | 6 | 1 (recruit) | 0 | Cannibalism (spellbook): can be placed in a battle where an enemy unit was killed. |
| ![](piece:wendigo:white) | **Wendigo** | Monster | 4 | 1 | 1 | Howl (spellbook): before a battle, forces the enemy to retreat a unit. Cannibalism (spellbook). |
| ![](piece:gnoph-keh:white) | **Gnoph-Keh** | Monster | 4 | as many as are in your pool | 3 | Berserkergang (spellbook): for each one assigned a Kill the enemy eliminates a Monster or Cultist. |
| ![](piece:rhan-tegoth:white) | **Rhan-Tegoth** | Great Old One | 1 | 6 | 3 | Eternal: pay 1 Power to cancel the Pain or Kill he receives (one result per battle). Awakens in any area marked with the Windwalker sigil. |
| ![](piece:ithaqua:white) | **Ithaqua** | Great Old One | 1 | 6 | half the enemy's Doom | Awakens once Rhan-Tegoth has been awakened, in place of a Gate in a sigil area (you need not control it). Ferox: while he is in play your Cultists cannot be captured by Monsters. |

### Rhan-Tegoth and Ithaqua: awakening and abilities

**Rhan-Tegoth:** 1. Pay **6** Power. 2. He appears in an area marked with the Windwalker sigil (the Arctic Ocean or
Antarctica).

**Eternal** (Post-Battle): if Rhan-Tegoth receives a Pain or a Kill, you may pay 1 Power to cancel its effect on him.
He can only receive one combat result per battle.

**Ithaqua:** 1. Rhan-Tegoth has been awakened (he need not be in play). 2. A Gate must stand in an area marked with
the sigil — anyone's: you need not control it. 3. Pay **6** Power: Ithaqua replaces that Gate.

**Ferox** (Ongoing): while Ithaqua is in play, your Cultists cannot be captured by enemy Monsters or Terrors. They are
still vulnerable to enemy Great Old Ones.

### Playing it

- **Build up, then blitz.** The cult is slow to start: its Gnoph-Kehs get cheaper as they leave the pool, Rhan-Tegoth is a
  six-Power Great Old One that can wake at either pole, and Ithaqua grows with every Doom point the enemy collects.
- **Hibernate** when the others have woken Great Old Ones: you bank Power (at most as much again as you have) and keep
  all of it for Gather Power, when it is added to what you gather — so you lead the next round, or have Power to
  strike when the others are spent. While you sleep you take no actions — and cannot answer what the others do.
- **Ice Age** taxes an area for everyone else: put it where the enemy needs to go.
- The sigil areas matter three times: where you begin, where Rhan-Tegoth wakes, and the *other* pole is the place for
  one of your requirements (a Gate there, in an area in which you did not start).

## Sleeper

![Sleeper glyph|96](guide/glyphs/sl.svg "Sleeper's glyph — its sigil: shown where the cult begins on the map.")

**Start:** the area marked with its sigil (North America, North America West on the maps that split it) · **Ability:** Death from Below (Doom phase): place the lowest-cost Monster from your pool into any area that holds at least one of your units.

| | Unit | Class | Pieces | Cost | Combat | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](piece:cultist:orange) | **Acolyte Cultist** | Cultist | 6 | 1 (recruit) | 0 |  |
| ![](piece:wizard:orange) | **Wizard** | Monster | 2 | 1 | 0 | Energy Nexus (spellbook): just before a battle in its area you may take an Action that starts there. |

| ![](piece:formless-spawn:orange) | **Formless Spawn** | Monster | 4 | 3 | one die per Formless Spawn on the map (+1 with Tsathoggua), each | Awakens Tsathoggua where it stands. |

### Tsathoggua: awakening and ability

1. You must have a **Formless Spawn** on the map.
2. Pay **8** Power.
3. Tsathoggua appears in the area of a Formless Spawn.

**Lethargy** (Action, cost 0): if Tsathoggua is in play, do nothing. This counts as an Action.

### Playing it

- **Wait.** The Sleeper does best when the others are exhausted: *Lethargy* lets it pass the turn without giving
  Power away, and *Tsathoggua*'s Combat is as large as your enemy's pile of Power.
- Three requirements are *actions* — Boon, Bestow, Bane — each paying 3 Power; do them when they cost the others
  something (Bane) or buy goodwill (Boon, Bestow).
- **Cursed Slumber** keeps a Gate and its Cultist safe from every ability and spellbook while they still give Doom and
  Power — and a Wizard in a battle area lets you act before any blow falls.

## Opener of the Way

### Yog-Sothoth: awakening

1. You must have a **Spawn of Yog-Sothoth** on the map.
2. Pay **6** Power. Yog-Sothoth replaces the Spawn.

### Playing it

- **Gates are your weapon.** The Beyond One drags an enemy's Gate (and the Acolyte on it) next to your army — taking it costs a Move, and the owner must answer. It cannot be used where an enemy Great Old One stands.
- **They Break Through** summons Monsters at enemy or abandoned Gates: reinforce a battle without walking there.
- The Opener has no way to earn Elder Signs but its Great Old One's Ritual signs: it wins with **Dragon Descending** — twice the Ritual's Doom, once — when many Gates are yours.

## Tcho-Tcho

![Tcho-Tcho glyph|96](guide/glyphs/tt.svg "Tcho-Tcho's glyph — its sigil: shown where the cult begins on the map.")

**Start:** an empty area marked with a faction glyph, after all the others have set up · **Ability:** Sycophancy (Doom phase): when an enemy performs a Ritual of Annihilation, either you gain 1 Doom or he earns 1 fewer — his choice.

| | Unit | Class | Pieces | Cost | Combat | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](piece:cultist:brown) | **Acolyte Cultist** | Cultist | 6 | 1 (recruit) | 0 | |
| ![](piece:high-priest:brown) | **High Priest** | Cultist | 3 | 3 (recruit) | 0 | Recruited like an Acolyte; gives 1 Power, holds a Gate, can be captured. Unspeakable Oath. With the High Priests option one Acolyte starts as a High Priest. |
| ![](piece:proto-shoggoth:brown) | **Proto-Shoggoth** | Monster | 6 | 2 | 1 | Terror (spellbook): each lowers the enemy's Combat or raises yours by 1. |
| ![](piece:ubbo-sathla:brown) | **Ubbo-Sathla** | Great Old One | 1 | 6 (0 in the Doom phase) | the Growth counter | **Hell's Banquet:** after he was awakened, in each Doom phase — whether or not he is in play — roll a die and add it to the Growth counter. |

### Ubbo-Sathla: awakening

1. You must control a **Gate** and have a **High Priest** in play (he need not be with the Gate), in the Doom phase or the Action phase.
2. In the **Doom phase** pay **0** Power; in the Action phase pay **6**.
3. Eliminate the High Priest; Ubbo-Sathla appears at your controlled Gate.

His Combat is the Growth counter, which starts at 0, grows by a die each Doom phase after his awakening, and by 1 whenever *Hierophants* has no High Priest left to place.

### Playing it

- Your Power comes from **Hierophants** (a High Priest for every spellbook) and Sycophancy (every Ritual of the others feeds you).
- Awaken Ubbo in the **Doom phase**: it costs nothing, and the counter starts feeding in the next one.
- The Tsang tribe is played here: **Hierophants, Soulless, Terror, Idolatry, Martyrdom** and **Tablets of the Gods**. (The Leng and Sarkomand tribes of Final Onslaught are not in the game.)

## The High Priests

An option chosen when the game is created. Every cult then has **one High Priest**, a unit of its own colour:

| | Unit | Class | Pieces | Cost | Combat | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](piece:high-priest:green) | **High Priest** | Cultist | 1 | 3 (recruit) | 0 | **Unspeakable Oath**: at the end of any player's Action, in Gather Power and in the Doom phase, sacrifice him (back to your pool) for 2 Power. |

He is a Cultist for every purpose: recruited like an Acolyte (for 3 Power), 1 Power in Gather Power, may build and hold a
Gate, may be captured (and goes home like any captive) and is a Cultist for *Zingaya*, *Ghroth*, Black Goat's *Blood
Sacrifice* and the like (an Acolyte is chosen first when both stand there), and Black Goat's *Frenzy* gives him 1
Combat. *Dreams* only works on Acolytes. A cult that loses him can recruit him again.
