# Battle

You may start a battle (cost **1 Power**) against one enemy cult in an area where **both** of you have units,
provided you have at least 1 **Combat**. A cult may battle once in each area per Action phase.

## The steps

1. **Declare** — choose the area and the enemy cult. The Sleeper's **Energy Nexus** may act now, and Cthulhu's
   **Devour** triggers.
2. **Pre-battle** — the attacker then the defender may use pre-battle spellbooks (Absorb, Abduct, Invisibility,
   Seek and Destroy, Howl, and the Sleeper's *Demand Sacrifice*, which asks the enemy first).
3. **Roll** — each side rolls as many dice as its **Combat**; results hit the *other* side.
4. **Assign** — the player who suffers results decides which of their units takes each one (Kills first). Great
   Cthulhu's *Regenerate* lets a Star Spawn take two; the Windwalker's *Rhan-Tegoth* can shrug a result off (*Eternal*).
5. **Post-battle** — spellbooks that act once the results are in: Berserkergang, Cannibalism, Necrophagy (which adds
   a Pain per Ghoul called, to units that took nothing yet), and Nyarlathotep's *Harbinger*, which pays for Great Old
   Ones that were Pained or Killed.
6. **Retreat** — units that took a Pain retreat.

## Dice

| Die | Result | Effect on a unit |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | **Kill** | The unit is eliminated (returned to its owner's pool). |
| 4–5 | **Pain** | The unit must **retreat** to an adjacent area. |
| 1–3 | Miss | Nothing. |

All **Kills** are assigned first, then all **Pains**. A unit cannot take two Pains, and a unit that has been
Killed is gone before it can be Pained.

## Combat values

| Unit | Combat |
|---|---|
| Cultists | 0 (1 for Black Goat with *Frenzy*) |
| Deep One / Shoggoth / Star Spawn / Cthulhu | 1 / 2 / 3 / 6 |
| Nightgaunt / Flying Polyp / Hunting Horror | 0 / 1 / 2 |
| Nyarlathotep | the spellbooks on your faction card plus your enemy's in the battle |
| Ghoul / Fungi / Dark Young | 0 / 1 / 2 |
| Shub-Niggurath | Gates you control + Cultists you have in play |
| Undead | one fewer than the number of Undead in the battle |
| Byakhee | one more than the number of Byakhee in the battle |
| Hastur | the current cost of a Ritual of Annihilation |
| King in Yellow | 0 |
| Wendigo / Gnoph-Keh / Rhan-Tegoth | 1 / 3 / 3 |
| Ithaqua | half of the enemy's Doom, rounded up |
| Wizard / Serpent Man | 0 / 1 |
| Formless Spawn | one die for every Formless Spawn on the map, and one more while Tsathoggua is in play (each) |
| Tsathoggua | 2, or the enemy's Power when that is more |
| Neutral Monsters: Dimensional Shambler / Star Vampire / Elder Thing / Servitor | 2 / 1 / 2 / −1 |
| Moonbeast / Gnorri / Shantak / Wamp / Voonith | 0 / 2 / 2 / 0 / 1 |
| Hound of Tindalos (a Terror) | 4 |
| Abhoth | the number of Filth Tokens in play (a Filth has 0) |
| Chaugnar Faugn | 3 — and +3 dice for his owner in any battle at a Gate it controls (Miri Nigri) |
| Cthugha | the Combat of an enemy Great Old One in the battle (the best one) |
| Mother Hydra | 6 minus the number of enemy units in the area, at least 1 |
| Yig | 2 |

Add up every unit of yours in the area. A side with Combat 0 rolls nothing. Rolling **6 or more dice** in one battle
meets a requirement of the Sleeper.

## The expansion cults in battle

- **Howl** (Windwalker, pre-battle): with a Wendigo present the enemy retreats one unit of *its* choice out of the
  area. It is not a Pain: the unit may go to an area where the Windwalker has units.
- **Demand Sacrifice** (Sleeper, pre-battle): while Tsathoggua is in play, before a battle with the Sleeper its enemy
  chooses — the Sleeper gains an Elder Sign, *or* all that enemy's Kills against the Sleeper's units count as Pains.
- **Energy Nexus** (Sleeper): just before a battle in an area with a Wizard, the Sleeper may take one Action that
  starts in that area, for its normal cost. The battle then goes on from its pre-battle abilities.
- **Eternal** (Windwalker, Rhan-Tegoth): if he is assigned a Pain or a Kill, the Windwalker may pay 1 Power to cancel
  it. He takes only one result per battle.
- **Berserkergang** (Windwalker, post-battle): for each Gnoph-Keh assigned a Kill, the enemy must eliminate one of its
  own Monsters or Cultists — its choice, anywhere on the map (never a Great Old One).
- **Cannibalism** (Windwalker, post-battle): once the results are applied, if one or more enemy units were killed, place
  a Wendigo or an Acolyte from your pool into the battle area — even in a battle you are not in. It takes no results.

## The Loyalty Cards in battle

- **Vampirism** (Star Vampire): its dice are rolled separately; each Pain drains 1 Power from the enemy and each Kill
  1 Doom, which you receive.
- **Vicious** (Voonith): the Kills the dice gave are raised to the number of Vooniths in the battle.
- **Angles of Time** (Hound of Tindalos): no Kill can be assigned to it; it is eliminated where no Gate stands.
- **Fire Vampires** (Cthugha, post-battle): once all results are assigned, the Kills on the enemy's units wait: you may
  spare any of them (a Pain instead, 1 Power each — and 1 Elder Sign each with Firestorm) unless the enemy has no
  Power or hibernates. The rest die.
- **Snakebite** (Yig, post-battle): if Cultists of yours were Killed the enemy takes one extra Kill, assigned to its own
  units.
- **Shriveling**, **Devour**, **Abduct**, **Absorb**, **Invisibility** and **Avatar** name *Monsters and Cultists*: a
  Terror is neither, and neither is a Great Old One.

## Retreating

A Pained unit moves to an **adjacent area that holds no enemy unit of the other side of the battle**; its owner
chooses (Crawling Chaos' *Madness* lets *it* choose for everyone — in any order, in battles it is not in too). If a side's pained units have nowhere to go,
the side eliminates one of them instead.

> [!TIP]
> **Capture** is not a battle: it costs 1 Power and needs no dice. If you can capture, you remove a Cultist
> that can never fight back — and each captive pays you 1 Power at Gather Power.

## Worked example

**Great Cthulhu** attacks **Black Goat** in the South Pacific. Cthulhu *devours* a Fungus before any dice are
rolled. Great Cthulhu has Cthulhu (6) and two Deep Ones (1 each): **8 dice**. Black Goat has a Dark Young (2)
and the Fungus that was devoured: **2 dice**.

Great Cthulhu rolls one 6 and one 5 among its eight dice: Black Goat suffers one Kill and one Pain. Black Goat
rolls a 6 and a 2: Great Cthulhu suffers one Kill.

Black Goat assigns its Kill to the Dark Young — the only unit it has left, so the Pain finds nothing to hit.
Great Cthulhu assigns its Kill to a Deep One. The remaining Cultists of both sides are unaffected.
