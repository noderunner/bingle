# Neutral cards

Some things in Cthulhu Wars belong to no cult. Any cult can take them, once, and keep them for the rest of the game:

- the **neutral spellbooks** of the Azathoth faction, which take the place of one of a cult's own books;
- **Loyalty Cards**: a card buys a whole set of figures — **neutral Monsters** and **Terrors** — and from then on only its buyer may summon and control them;
- the **Independent Great Old Ones**, which have a page of their own ([Independents](tab:independents)).

Everything on this page is optional. On the setup page switch on the packs you want in the box:

| Option | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **Azathoth (neutral spellbooks and Monsters)** | the six neutral spellbooks and four Monsters: Dimensional Shambler, Star Vampire, Elder Thing, Servitor |
| **Dreamlands Surface Monsters** | three Monsters: Moonbeast, Gnorri, Shantak |
| **Beyond Time and Space** | two Monsters, Wamp and Voonith, and the Terror Hound of Tindalos |
| **Independent: …** | Independent Great Old Ones, one check box each (see [Independents](tab:independents)) |

Besides, a cult that has Ghroth or The Thousand Forms may swap that book for a neutral one at the setup page even without the Azathoth option (see below).

## Neutral spellbooks

The rules of the Azathoth faction, as the box prints them:

> These Spellbooks are unique gameplay items. When you fulfill one of your Faction's Spellbook requirements, you may take an Azathoth Spellbook instead of a Faction Spellbook from your Pool.
>
> Once you take a Neutral Spellbook, it is yours; no one else can earn it. You retain that Spellbook throughout the game (exception: *Recriminations*). This means that you will NOT have access to one of your Faction Spellbooks in this game, so choose wisely.
>
> A Neutral Spellbook counts as one of the 6 Spellbooks needed on your Faction Card to allow you to perform Unlimited Battle and win the game, as well as counting for any other rules or abilities that reference your Faction Card's Spellbooks (such as Nyarlathotep's Combat rating or the Moonbeasts' ability).

*Remember:* Azathoth's own Spellbook, **Nuclear Chaos**, is **not** a Neutral Spellbook — it can only be placed on Azathoth's Loyalty Card by the player who controls Azathoth (an Independent Great Old One). It can never be placed on a player's Faction Card. Azathoth himself is not part of this game.

How Bingle plays it: when a requirement earns you a book the choice lists your own books you do not hold yet *and* the neutral books nobody holds. The neutral book goes on your faction card; it counts in the *Spellbooks 4/6* counter of the card, in the six you need for Unlimited Battle and the game, in Nyarlathotep's Combat (his and his enemy's books) and in the cult's other rules that count books. Since the cult earns six books at most, taking a neutral one means one of its own is never taken.

| Spellbook | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Shriveling** | Pre-Battle | Select an enemy Monster or Cultist in the battle. That unit is eliminated, and its owner receives Power equal to its cost. Once a battle. It names Monsters and Cultists: a Terror or a Great Old One cannot be chosen. |
| **Stars Are Right** | Ongoing | In the Doom phase, when you turn in Elder Signs for Doom, you also receive Power equal to their face value. |
| **Recriminations** | Action: Cost 1 | Remove any spellbook (this one too) from your faction card and replace it with another available spellbook. |
| **The Mao Ceremony** | Ongoing | At the end of Gather Power, after the Power has been added and before the first player is determined, you may sacrifice 1 or more of your own Cultists: 1 Power each. |
| **Umr at-Tawil** | Ongoing | Gates cost you 2 Power to build. |
| **Undimensioned** | Action: Cost 2 | Rearrange your units among their areas as you see fit. You may empty an area completely, but you may not move into any new area. (Filth Tokens never move.) |

- **Recriminations** sets one book aside and asks you at once which book takes its place; a book that is set aside goes back — an own book can be earned again, a neutral one is free for anyone. A Moonbeast lying on the book goes back to its owner's pool.
- **Undimensioned** works with the areas where you have units *when you start it*; click a unit, then the area it goes to, as often as you like, and press Done. The unit that stands on a Gate stays on it.
- **The Mao Ceremony** asks you after Gather Power, click a Cultist to sacrifice it for 1 Power (it returns to your pool); press Done when you have had enough. A sacrificed Cultist that held a Gate abandons it.

### Instead of Ghroth or The Thousand Forms

Black Goat's **Ghroth** gives its enemies a minute to decide who loses Cultists, and Crawling Chaos' **The Thousand Forms** is a negotiation: both are awkward when the turns are taken days apart. On the setup page, once Black Goat or Crawling Chaos sits at the table, you may **replace** that book with one of the six neutral spellbooks — *even without the Azathoth option*. The cult then has five books of its own and the neutral one, which is set aside for it: it is earned like any other and nobody else may take it. Each neutral spellbook can replace one book only.

### Strategy (from the wiki)

- **Shriveling:** snipes the toughest unit. Rarely worth it unless that unit is a lone meat shield for a high-value target, or gives a secondary effect — Formless Spawn or Dark Young, that add to the other units' dice. Risky: the Power freed often benefits the victim.
- **Recriminations:** replaces single-use books such as Dragon Ascending and Dragon Descending once they are used (they stay face down even when chosen again), and combos with the Tcho-Tcho's Hierophants — a freed slot is another free High Priest.
- **Stars Are Right:** for factions that go for Elder Signs (Black Goat, Yellow Sign). Best saved for the last rounds: it shows how much of a threat you are.
- **The Mao Ceremony:** best with cheap Cultists. Black Goat refunds Acolytes for up to three Ghroths; the Windwalker cashes them in and brings them back with Cannibalism. The best combo is Mother Hydra's Zygote: all Acolytes of the pool for 1 Power, a profit of up to 5.
- **Umr at-Tawil:** for Yellow Sign and Black Goat, whose Monsters cost 2 or less: two Gates and two Monsters in the first round. Vital for the Opener of the Way, whose "12 Gates" requirement is daunting.
- **Undimensioned:** regroups armies on the cheap; best with a way to sneak a unit into enemy land — Avatar, Necrophagy, Dreams, They Break Through, or a Flying Polyp with Invisibility. With Dimensional Shamblers (once Unlimited Battle is in hand) the Shamblers are an outpost and the army follows.

## Loyalty Cards

The rules, as printed:

> A player may purchase a Neutral Monster's Loyalty Card during the Doom Phase. A player may never purchase more than one of these in a given Doom Phase, but there is no limit to the number of Neutral Monster Loyalty Cards a player may possess.
>
> To purchase one of these Loyalty Cards, pay 2 Doom when it is your turn to perform a Ritual of Annihilation. If you wish, you may still perform the Ritual. When spending Doom, choose a Neutral Monster Loyalty Card from among those available, placing that card and its associated monsters by your Faction Card. You will typically have the opportunity to immediately place one of these new Monsters for free.
>
> Once you have purchased a Neutral Monster Loyalty Card, it is yours for the rest of the game. From then on, only you may summon and Control its associated Neutral Monsters.

In Bingle:

- In your turn of the **Doom phase** every card still in the pool of the packs in the game has a button: **Buy the … card (2 Doom)**. Buying costs no Ritual and does not end your turn: you may still perform the Ritual of Annihilation (and so pay for the card out of the Doom the Ritual just earned) or reveal your Elder Signs.
- **One Loyalty Card in a Doom phase** — Monster or Terror (a Terror and a Monster cannot be bought in the same phase) — and as many cards as you like over the game.
- The card's figures go to your **pool**; the card itself shows in your *Loyalty Cards* panel. You will usually place one for free (at a Gate you control), as the card says.
- Your units of that kind are summoned like any Monster (a button each, with its cost). Nobody else ever has them.

> [!NOTE]
> Mind Control stops, for an enemy Great Old One that shares its area with an Elder Thing: Devour, The Harbinger, Avatar, Desecrate, Vengeance, Eternal — and the abilities of the Independents (Filth, Miri Nigri, The Agony Sting, Fire Vampires, Snakebite). A Great Old One's dice and its spellbooks are not abilities and still count.

**Strategy (from the wiki).**
- *Dimensional Shambler:* instant muscle and meat shields. Placed after an enemy's move and before it declares a battle, they interrupt Unlimited Battle; they back up cults that put an expensive Great Old One in harm's way (Avatar, Dreams, He Who is Not to be Named) or that are stretched thin (Yellow Sign's Desecration tokens).
- *Star Vampire:* especially potent with the Opener of the Way's Channel Power.
- *Elder Thing:* cripples enemy Great Old Ones — the Harbinger, The Key and the Gate, Desecrate, Devour, Vengeance, Hell's Banquet — or removes their protection (Eternal, Ferox).
- *Servitor of the Outer Gods:* mainly a handicap for the chosen enemy; less effective against Yellow Sign, the Sleeper and the Windwalker, who have other ways to get Monsters on the board.

### Dreamlands Surface Monster Pack

| | Monster | Cost | Combat | Pieces | Ability |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](piece:moonbeast:green) | **Moonbeast** | 2 | 0 | 4 | **Blasphemous Obeisance** (Ongoing) |
| ![](piece:gnorri:green) | **Gnorri** | 3 | 2 | 3 | **Grottos** (Ongoing) |
| ![](piece:shantak:green) | **Shantak** | 2 | 2 | 2 | **Horror Steed** (Ongoing) |

**Moonbeast** — *Pay 2 Doom to obtain this Loyalty Card, then place 1 Moonbeast on an enemy Faction Spellbook.*
**Blasphemous Obeisance:** when a Moonbeast is summoned, place it on an enemy's Faction Spellbook. While the Moonbeast is there, the player cannot use that Spellbook (it still counts for other purposes such as Unlimited Battle and winning the game). The next Doom phase, place the Moonbeast on one of your Gates. A Moonbeast can be prematurely returned to the map if the victim spends 1 Doom point (at any time, not as an Action).

How Bingle plays it: buying the card or summoning a Moonbeast (2 Power, at a Gate you control) asks you which enemy spellbook it lies on — any spellbook an enemy holds, a neutral one too, that has no Moonbeast on it yet. (With no such book the Moonbeast cannot be summoned.) The book is shown with *a Moonbeast lies on it* and its buttons are gone. At the start of the next Doom phase every Moonbeast comes to one of its owner's Gates (a choice when there are several; with no Gate it returns to the pool). The victim may free the book earlier: **Pay 1 Doom: free …** is offered in its own turns and Doom decisions, no Action needed; the Moonbeast then comes to its owner's Gate at once. A book that is taken off the card (Recriminations) frees its Moonbeast too.

**Gnorri** — *Pay 2 Doom to obtain this Loyalty Card, plus place 1 Gnorri at your controlled Gate.* **Grottos:** during the Doom phase, if you have two Gnorri in play, you earn one extra Doom point. If you have three, you earn two. (Counted on the map at the start of the Doom phase; the Doom is shown in the log as *from Loyalty Cards*.)

**Shantak** — *Pay 2 Doom to obtain this Loyalty Card, plus place 1 Shantak at your controlled Gate.* **Horror Steed:** when moving a Shantak, it can reach any area on the map. In addition, the Shantak may carry one of your Cultists with it for free. (In a Move, a Shantak's step to any area costs 1 Power like any step; then one Cultist — or High Priest — that stands where the Shantak was may follow to the same area for nothing. Each Shantak offers one place.)

**Strategy.** The wiki has no strategy section for the Dreamlands Surface Monster Pack. How Bingle's bots use them: they buy the **Gnorri** early (when they have the Power to summon two or three and a long game ahead — a steady Doom income), the **Shantak** and the **Moonbeast** when Doom allows (a Moonbeast lies on the strongest book of the player leading in Doom), pay 1 Doom to free a book when they can afford it, and move a Shantak to guard a Gate that is in danger.

### Beyond Time and Space

| | Unit | Cost | Combat | Pieces | Ability |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](piece:wamp:green) | **Wamp** (Monster) | 1 | 0 | 4 | **Crypt Dweller** (Doom phase) |
| ![](piece:voonith:green) | **Voonith** (Monster) | 3 | 1 | 2 | **Vicious** (Post-Battle) |
| ![](piece:hound-of-tindalos:green) | **Hound of Tindalos** (Terror) | 4 | 4 | 1 | **Chronophage**, **Angles of Time** (Ongoing) |

**Wamp** — *Pay 2 Doom to obtain this Loyalty Card, then choose an enemy to place all 4 Wamps in any Area or Areas without a Gate.* **Crypt Dweller** (Doom phase): choose an enemy to place all Wamps from your pool into any area or areas without a Gate. (You may still summon Wamps normally.) In Bingle, buying the card and the button **Crypt Dweller** of your Doom turn (once a Doom phase, while Wamps are in your pool) ask you which enemy; that enemy then places the Wamps one by one: it clicks an area with no Gate, again and again until none is left. The Wamps are yours wherever they stand.

**Voonith** — *Pay 2 Doom to obtain this Loyalty Card, then place 1 Voonith at one of your Controlled Gates.* **Vicious** (Post-Battle): for each Kill you score fewer than the number of Vooniths involved in the Battle, add 1 Kill (with 2 Vooniths in the Battle and 1 Kill rolled, add 1 extra Kill for a total of 2; with no Kill rolled, add 2). In Bingle the Kills the dice gave are raised to the number of your Vooniths in the battle (after any rerolls), before they are assigned.

**Strategy.** The wiki has none for Wamp and Voonith. The bots buy a Voonith for its steady two Kills, and let an enemy place their Wamps only when many are in the pool.

## Terrors

The rules, as printed:

> Terrors are summoned as though they are Monsters, and require a Controlled Gate to be able to enter play. They are equal to Monsters in their ability to Capture Cultists. That is, Monsters can protect Cultists against Terrors (and vice versa), and Great Old Ones can still Capture Cultists protected by Terrors. However, as Terrors are a different type of Unit, they are not vulnerable to abilities that specifically target Monsters.
>
> To acquire a Terror, pay 2 Power and 2 Doom when it is your turn to perform a Ritual of Annihilation. You may still perform the Ritual, if you wish. Take your choice of available Terror Loyalty Cards and follow the instructions for placing your new figure on the Map.
>
> Players may only purchase a single Loyalty Card in any given Doom Phase, so they may not gain a Neutral Monster in the same Doom Phase in which they acquire a Terror (and vice versa).
>
> Once a Terror Loyalty Card has been acquired, it belongs to that player for the rest of the game. Only that player may Summon and control that Terror.

In Bingle a Terror stands between the Monsters and the Great Old Ones (its token is drawn a little bigger than a Monster's). It guards Cultists as a Monster does, can be summoned at a Gate you control, and is **not** a Monster for: Great Cthulhu's Devour, Crawling Chaos' Abduct and Invisibility, Shriveling, Absorb, Avatar, Berserkergang, Capture Monster, Death from Below, Desecrate and the Million Favored Ones. Of the Tcho-Tcho's, the Proto-Shoggoths are Monsters (their spellbook is only *called* Terror).

**Hound of Tindalos** — *Pay 2 Doom and 2 Power to obtain this Loyalty Card, then place the Hound at any Gate (even one you don't control).*

- **Chronophage:** the Hound cannot perform the Move action by itself. It moves for free whenever you move any other unit: it teleports directly from an area with a Gate to another area with a Gate, neither of which need be controlled. *In Bingle, once another unit has moved in your Move action, the Hound on a Gate may hop to any other Gate for nothing — once in a Move. The Move stays open while a hop is possible: press Done when you do not want it.*
- **Angles of Time:** the Hound cannot be assigned a Kill in Battle. But if a Hound is ever in an area without a Gate (Pained into one, or its Gate destroyed or moved) it is eliminated. It can also be eliminated if it cannot be Pained for want of a place (the usual rule). *In Bingle the check is made when an action or a battle is over, so a Gate removed or moved by the Beyond One takes the Hound with it.*

**Strategy.** The wiki has no strategy section for the Hound of Tindalos. Its four dice and invulnerability to Kills make it a fine guard for a Gate under threat; the bots buy it with two or more Gates and Power in hand, summon it where Cultists need a guard, and hop it to a Gate where their units stand or an enemy is near.

## Bots

The bots buy a card when it is worth 2 Doom (see above) and the game is long enough for it, use the cards' abilities as described, and leave Recriminations and Undimensioned to people; they take neutral spellbooks only when nothing of their own is left.
