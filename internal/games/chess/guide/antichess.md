# Antichess

Antichess turns chess inside out: you **win by losing all your pieces**, or by being left with no legal move.

- **Captures are compulsory.** If you can capture, you must; if there are several captures, choose any of them.
- **The king is an ordinary piece.** There is no check and no checkmate, the king may be captured like any other piece, and there is no castling.
- A pawn reaching the last rank may become a **king** as well as a queen, rook, bishop or knight.

The game is drawn by the same position three times and after fifty moves each without a capture or a pawn move.
