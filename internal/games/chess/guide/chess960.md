# Chess960

Chess960 (Fischer Random Chess) is chess with the pieces of the first rank shuffled before the game: one of 960 starting arrangements, drawn at random. The bishops always stand on squares of both colours and the king somewhere between the two rooks, and Black's pieces mirror White's. The pawns stand where they always do.

Every rule is the rule of chess but castling.

## Castling

Castling ends with the king and rook on the squares they would reach in chess, wherever they started:

- **king's side** (`O-O`): the king on the g file and the rook on the f file;
- **queen's side** (`O-O-O`): the king on the c file and the rook on the d file.

Neither may have moved, every square between where they start and where they end must be empty but for the two of them, and the king may not be in check, cross an attacked square or land on one. The king may move several squares, one, or not at all.

To castle, click the **king** and then the **rook**.
