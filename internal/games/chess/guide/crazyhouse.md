# Crazyhouse

Crazyhouse is chess in which nothing is lost for good: a piece you capture joins your **hand**, turned to your color, and you can bring it back.

## Dropping

On your turn you may either move a piece, or **drop** one from your hand onto any empty square. To drop, choose the piece in your hand and then the square. In the notation a drop is the piece's letter, `@` and the square: `N@f3`, `P@e4`.

- A pawn may not be dropped on the first or the last rank.
- A piece that was promoted goes to the hand as a **pawn** when it is captured.
- A drop may block a check, give check, or even give checkmate.

## The end

The game is won by checkmate and drawn by stalemate and by the same position three times, with the same pieces in hand. There is no fifty-move rule, and a game never runs out of material.
