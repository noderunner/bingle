# Atomic Chess

In Atomic chess every capture is an **explosion**. The piece taken, the piece taking it and every piece **but a pawn** on the eight squares around are removed from the board, whichever side they belong to. En passant explodes on the square the capturing pawn reaches.

## Winning

You win by **blowing up the other king**: capture a piece next to it. You can also win by checkmate.

- A king may not capture.
- You may not make a capture that blows up your own king, even if it blows up the other one too.
- Two kings standing side by side cannot check each other: taking one would blow up the other.

The game is drawn by stalemate, by the same position three times, after fifty moves each without a capture or a pawn move, and when only the kings are left.
