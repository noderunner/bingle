# The tiles

Every tile moves in its own way. All moves keep the hive in one piece and obey [freedom to move](tab:moving).

## Queen Bee

![](piece:queen:white) ![](piece:queen:black)

**Moves 1 space** by sliding.

The Queen is the heart of the game: if she is surrounded, you lose. She is slow, so protect her — but she must be placed by your fourth turn, and nothing else may move until she is down.

## Beetle

![](piece:beetle:white) ![](piece:beetle:black)

**Moves 1 space** — and may **climb on top of the hive**.

A Beetle can step onto an occupied space, covering the tile there; from the top it may step to another occupied space or back down to the ground. The tile it covers cannot move while it is covered, and the stack counts as the colour of the tile **on top**: for placing new tiles, a stack topped by your Beetle is yours.

::: tip
A Beetle on top of an enemy Queen pins her where she stands — though the Queen still counts as surrounded as usual if all six neighbours are occupied.
:::

## Spider

![](piece:spider:white) ![](piece:spider:black)

**Moves exactly 3 spaces**, by sliding, around the outside of the hive.

It may not turn back on itself: it cannot enter a space it has already passed through during the move, so it always ends exactly three spaces from where it began along its path.

## Grasshopper

![](piece:grasshopper:white) ![](piece:grasshopper:black)

**Jumps in a straight line** over one or more tiles to the first empty space.

It must jump over at least one tile, in one of the six directions, and lands on the first empty space it reaches. It ignores the freedom-to-move rule (it flies), but the One Hive rule still applies.

## Soldier Ant

![](piece:ant:white) ![](piece:ant:black)

**Slides any number of spaces** around the outside of the hive.

The Ant is the strongest tile: it can reach almost any open space on the edge of the hive in one move. It must slide the whole way (obeying freedom to move) and end on a different space from where it began.

## Mosquito (expansion)

![](piece:mosquito:white) ![](piece:mosquito:black)

**Moves like any tile it touches** at the moment it moves (not another Mosquito).

If the Mosquito touches an Ant, it may move like an Ant; touching a Spider and a Grasshopper, it may choose either. It takes its powers from the tiles around it, so it changes every turn. If it is on top of the hive it can only move like a **Beetle**. A Mosquito next to a **Pillbug** may also use the Pillbug's throw.

## Ladybug (expansion)

![](piece:ladybug:white) ![](piece:ladybug:black)

**Moves exactly 3 spaces**: two **over the top of the hive**, then **one down** onto an empty space.

Both of the first two steps land on occupied spaces (a different one each time), and the last goes down to the ground. It cannot end where it began.

## Pillbug (expansion)

![](piece:pillbug:white) ![](piece:pillbug:black)

**Moves 1 space** like the Queen — **or** throws a tile next to it.

Instead of moving, the Pillbug may pick up a single tile that **touches** it (yours or your opponent's) and drop it on an empty space that also touches the Pillbug, over the Pillbug's back. The throw follows several limits:

- the tile may not be one that was **just moved** by the opponent's last turn;
- the tile must be **alone** on its space (no stacks) and its removal must not split the hive;
- the Pillbug itself must not be covered by a Beetle, and the lift and drop obey the height version of freedom to move.

::: tip
A Pillbug can throw an enemy tile away from your Queen — or into a worse place for your opponent.
:::
