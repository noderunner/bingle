# Hive

John Yianni's classic insect game, with the three expansion insects (Mosquito, Ladybug and Pillbug) in the default game. There is no board: the tiles you place *are* the board, a single connected **hive** that grows and shifts as the game goes on. Surround your opponent's **Queen Bee** on all six sides to win.

## The goal

You win when the enemy Queen Bee is completely surrounded — all six neighbouring spaces occupied, by tiles of **either** colour. If one move surrounds both Queens, the game is a draw. If your own move surrounds only your own Queen, you lose.

## A turn

On your turn you must do one of two things:

1. **Place** a tile from your supply next to the hive, or
2. **Move** a tile that is already in the hive, as its kind allows.

If you can do neither, you pass.

### Placing tiles

- White's first tile starts the hive; Black's first tile must touch it.
- After that, a newly placed tile may touch **only your own tiles** (the top tile of a stack counts). It must touch at least one tile.
- **Your Queen Bee must be placed by your fourth turn** (your fourth placement or move; before then you may place it whenever you like).
- **No tile may move until your Queen is down.**

### Moving tiles

All moves obey two rules, explained in [moving](tab:moving):

- the **One Hive** rule: the hive must stay in one piece, always;
- **freedom to move**: a tile must be able to slide into its destination.

## In the game

Click a tile in your supply, then a highlighted space to place it. Click a tile on the table, then a highlighted space to move it. There is no board drawn: only the tiles and the spaces you can go to appear, and the picture zooms to fit the hive. A tile standing on others shows a number (how high the stack is).
