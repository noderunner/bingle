# 0046. Cthulhu Wars: Loyalty Cards (neutral Monsters, Terrors) and the Independent Great Old Ones; faction glyphs

Status: accepted (2026-10-25)

## Context

The player's playtest list asked for the official Loyalty Card rules (neutral Monsters, Terrors, Independent Great Old Ones), the neutral spellbook rules, three more packs — the Dreamlands Surface Monster Pack (Moonbeast, Gnorri, Shantak), Great Old One Pack 1 (Abhoth, Chaugnar Faugn, Cthugha, Mother Hydra, Yig) and Beyond Time and Space (Wamp, Voonith, the Hound of Tindalos) — and the real glyph (sigil) of every faction, in the UI, on the map and in the rules.

## Decision

- **One registry of cards** (`loyalty.go`): `cards` lists the 15 Loyalty Cards in the order of the bits of `Data.Loyal` (now `uint16`): the Azathoth faction's four Monsters (unchanged numbers), then Moonbeast, Gnorri, Shantak, Wamp, Voonith, the Hound of Tindalos (a Terror), and the five Independents. Their figures are unit types appended after the Opener's and the Tcho-Tcho's (`unitTypes`, `ActionNotations` and the JSON `pieces`/`reserves` only grow at the end); Abhoth's twelve Filth Tokens are a unit type too (`filthSpec`, `Token: true`: never summoned or moved). `e.units(seat)` lists the figures of the cards a seat holds, so the conservation check, the views and the move generators need no special case.
- **Packs are options**: the bools `dreamlands`, `beyond` and one `igoo-<name>` per Independent (the wiki recommends one Independent fewer than the players), part of the derived id (`-dl`, `-bt`, `-g1acthy` — letters for the Independents) and of `params`; `SeatCultOptions` keeps them.
- **Buying** (`buyMoves`/`buy`): 2 Doom in the Doom turn for a Monster, 2 Doom and 2 Power for a Terror; **one Loyalty Card in a Doom phase** whatever its kind (`DoomState.Bought`); the Ritual may still be performed. Independents are not bought: **awakening** is an Action at a controlled Gate (Yog-Sothoth counts) in an area with a Great Old One of the seat — `awaken:abhoth@area`; Cthugha's carries the Great Old One he replaces (`awaken:cthugha@area/cthulhu`, `Move.Count` = unit number + 1) and costs 6 minus what that one costs now (negative: Power gained). The card, its spellbook (`Data.IBooks`) and its tokens go to the seat; **death** (`eliminate` → `igooDies`) puts them back.
- **Terror** (`classTerror`, drawn between Monster and Great Old One): guards like a Monster, is summoned like one, and is not a "Monster" for the abilities that name Monsters and Cultists (`isMonsterOrCultist`: Devour, Abduct, Absorb, Invisibility, Avatar, Shriveling, Berserkergang…). The Hound hops from Gate to Gate for free once another unit has moved in a Move (`houndHops`, once per Move), cannot be assigned a Kill and is eliminated where no Gate stands (`angles`, after every action and when a pained Hound cannot retreat).
- **Moonbeast**: `Data.Moon` holds the beasts lying on spellbooks; `Data.hasBook`/`hasNeutral` say *usable* (not mooned) while `holds`/`heldNeutral`/`bookCount` say *held*, so a mooned book still counts for Unlimited Battle, the win and Nyarlathotep. The beasts come back at the start of the next Doom phase (`moonReturn`); the victim may pay 1 Doom (`ransom:<book>`) in its own turns and Doom decisions. **Gnorri**: extra Doom at the start of the Doom phase (`DoomLedger.Cards`). **Shantak**: reach everywhere (`reachFor`), `MoveState.Carry` gives a Cultist a free ride between the same two areas. **Wamp**: the buyer (or the Doom-turn action `crypt:<key>`) names an enemy, who places them one by one in Gate-free areas. **Voonith**: the Kills of the dice are raised to the number of Vooniths in the battle.
- **Independents' mechanics**: Abhoth's Filth is an Action (`filth@area`); The Brood is `doomGateCount` (Doom scoring and Rituals; not Gather Power); Chaugnar's Miri Nigri is in `combatOf`, his requirement is `discard:<pair>`, and **the Curse** makes `gainSign` leave a pending value (a `0` in `Data.Signs`, the seat in `Data.Picks`) that the execute epilogue (`openPicks`) turns into a `pick` prompt for the Curse's holder at the front of the queue — whatever flow gave the sign; Cthugha's **Fire Vampires** keep Killed units in `Battle.Doomed` until the attacker's `spare` prompt is over (`finishAssign`/`afterKills`, `killUnit`); Mother Hydra's Sting is a prompt per enemy (`sting:<unit>>area`), the Zygote an action followed by one click per Acolyte; Yig's Snakebite is an extra Kill assigned after the results (`Battle.Venom`), his requirement `gateoff@area`, Messenger of Yig a `donate` prompt for every other seat after the Gates have scored. State-based requirements (Abhoth's, Mother Hydra's) are met in `checkRequirements`, event-based ones where the event happens (`noteKill`, `discard`, `gateOff`).
- **Combat**: the Great Old One dice moved out of `combatOf`'s per-cult switch into `gooDice`, so Cthugha can copy an enemy Great Old One's.
- **Glyphs**: `scripts/cwglyphs.py` traces the wiki's sigils into `guide/glyphs/<key>.svg` and `js/cw-glyphs.js`; the map draws the glyph of every cult at its start area (and the Windwalker's and Sleeper's sigil areas) instead of the old diamonds; the Factions page shows each, with traced placeholders for the Ancients, Bubastis, Daemon Sultan, the Invasion and Azathoth (the Bad Movie Dimension is left out).
- **Bots** (`guide_cards.go`): they buy cards worth 2 Doom (`cardBuyScore`), awaken Independents (Cthugha only over Cthulhu or Ubbo-Sathla — the wiki's combos), place Filth as guards, use the Zygote, spare Great Old Ones, give Power to Yig, and answer every new prompt.

## Interpretations

Where the wiki is silent: a Moonbeast may lie on any held spellbook of an enemy (neutral ones too), once per book, and cannot be summoned when there is none; "at any time" for the ransom is the victim's own turns and Doom decisions. Wamps are placed one at a time by the chosen enemy. The Hound hops once per Move after another unit has moved; Angles of Time is checked at the end of an action. A Pained Filth retreats like any unit. Filth left on the map after Abhoth's death keeps its old owner. Yig's Snakebite needs Yig on the map; a cult with no Power cannot give for Messenger of Yig and counts as refusing. Discarded Elder Signs go back into the bag. Cthugha's Combat is the best enemy Great Old One in the battle (his owner's choice). Great Cthulhu's Immortal pays an Elder Sign for an Independent too. Independents give no Elder Sign in a Ritual (`gooInPlay` leaves them out; `gooTotal` counts them for Hibernate and Yog-Sothoth). Azathoth himself and the other Independents are not implemented.
